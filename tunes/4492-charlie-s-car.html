Charlie's Car

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Charlie's Car is a modern Irish reel composed by well-known button accordion and flute player Peter Browne. Written in the key of E minor and set in 4/4 meter, the tune follows the lively and rhythmic structure characteristic of traditional Irish dance music. As a contemporary composition, it is frequently played in sessions by musicians exploring modern additions to the traditional repertoire.
