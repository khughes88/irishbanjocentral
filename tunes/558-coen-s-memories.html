Coen's Memories

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

Coen's Memories is an Irish reel composed by the well-known fiddler Tommy Coen from County Galway. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the E dorian mode, the tune features the lively and fluid phrasing characteristic of East Galway fiddle music. Because specific historical associations beyond Coen's own authorship are not widely documented, the tune is best appreciated through its vibrant regional style and enduring popularity in traditional sessions.
