Beauty Of The North

Strathspey · 4/4 · C dorian

Beauty of the North is a traditional strathspey composed by Simon Fraser. Set in C Dorian with a 4/4 meter, the tune exemplifies the characteristic dotted rhythms and energetic drive of traditional Scottish fiddle music. As part of the Fraser collection heritage, it remains a celebrated piece within the northern Scottish repertoire.
