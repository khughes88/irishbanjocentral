Seonaidh Mor

Barndance · 4/4 · D minor

Seonaidh Mor is a contemporary barndance composed in 4/4 meter and D minor by Scottish fiddler Jonny Hardie of the band Old Blind Dogs. The tune is driven by a lively, rhythmic bounce that is characteristic of modern Scottish dance music and folk arrangements. Because it was written by a known contemporary musician, it lacks the obscure origins of traditional tunes and is frequently performed in modern Celtic music sessions.
