Dumbarton's Drums

Reel · 4/4 · B minor

Dumbarton's Drums is a traditional Scottish tune of uncertain historical origin that first appeared in print during the early eighteenth century. It is traditionally played with a steady, moderate tempo and a lyrical yet rhythmic character suited to both listening and dancing. The melody has historical associations with the Royal Regiment of Scots Royals, historically commanded by the Earl of Dumbarton, whose drummers reportedly used it as a marching tune.
