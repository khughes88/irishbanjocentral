Dr Finlay's Casebook

March · 4/4 · G major

Dr Finlay's Casebook is a 4/4 march in G major composed by Trevor Duncan as the theme music for the classic television series of the same name. The tune is characterized by its bright, steady, and uplifting rhythmic drive, making it well suited for brass and light orchestral performance. Its primary historical association remains its enduring popularity as the recognizable signature tune for the BBC television adaptation set in the Scottish Highlands.
