Confinement, The

Jig · 6/8 · A minor

The jig known as The Confinement was composed by Pierre Commes. Written in six-eight meter and set in A minor, the tune features an energetic and driving rhythm characteristic of traditional French-Canadian and Acadian fiddle music. Because specific historical associations and detailed provenance for the piece remain largely undocumented, its enduring appeal rests entirely on its lively melodic character and engaging dance tempo.
