Ding Dong Merrily On High

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Ding Dong Merrily On High is a well-known Christmas carol rather than a traditional instrumental reel, with its famous melody first published in 1924 by George Ratcliffe Woodward alongside harmonies by Charles Wood. Despite being listed here with a 4/4 meter and a D major key signature, the tune is traditionally sung in triple meter as a joyous and festive hymn. Because its historical background is rooted in a 16th-century French secular dance tune rather than indigenous folk tradition, detailed performance practices vary widely across modern arrangements.
