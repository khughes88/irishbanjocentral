Bushes And Briars

Waltz · 3/4 · A minor

Bushes And Briars is a traditional English folk song and tune that was notably collected by Ralph Vaughan Williams from the singing of Charles Potiphar in Essex. Set in A minor with a 3/4 meter, this waltz is traditionally played with a gentle, flowing character that suits its lyrical origins. While the tune's deeper history remains uncertain, Pete Cunningham is credited as the composer of this specific contemporary arrangement for the piece.
