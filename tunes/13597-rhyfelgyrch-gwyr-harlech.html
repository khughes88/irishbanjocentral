Rhyfelgyrch Gwŷr Harlech

March · 4/4 · G major

Rhyfelgyrch Gwŷr Harlech, commonly known as Men of Harlech, is a traditional Welsh march with origins that are historically uncertain beyond its deep association with Welsh musical heritage. Set in 4/4 meter and G major, the tune is characterized by a bold, stirring, and resolute martial character well suited for foot soldiers. It is historically linked in popular tradition to the lengthy fifteenth-century siege of Harlech Castle, though this specific connection lacks definitive documentary proof.
