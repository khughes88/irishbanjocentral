Planxty George Brabazon

March · 4/4 · G major

Composed by the legendary blind harpist Turlough O'Carolan, Planxty George Brabazon is a well-known Irish tune dedicated to a member of the prominent Brabazon family. Written in G major and traditionally performed in a lively 4/4 meter, the piece exhibits the characteristic graceful yet spirited melodic flow typical of O'Carolan's polite compositions. While specific historical dates and performance contexts for its creation remain undocumented, the tune has endured as a celebrated standard in the traditional Irish repertoire.
