Packie Byrne's

Jig · 6/8 · E minor

Packie Byrne's is an Irish traditional jig in 6/8 meter and E minor, named after the renowned Donegal fiddler and tin whistle player Packie Byrne. Because specific historical records regarding the tune's exact composition date and origin are uncertain, its background remains rooted in the oral tradition of itinerant musicians.
