Flower Of Scotland, The

Jig · 6/8 · G major

The Flower Of Scotland is a modern Scottish patriotic song and tune written by Roy Williamson of the folk group The Corries in the 1960s. Written in G major with a 6/8 meter, the piece functions as a lively jig featuring a stirring and anthemic character that is widely performed by a marching band style instrumentation. It has since become one of Scotland's most prominent unofficial national anthems, frequently performed before international rugby and football matches.
