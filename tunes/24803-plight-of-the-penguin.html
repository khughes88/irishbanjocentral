Plight Of The Penguin

Jig · 6/8 · A major

Plight of the Penguin is a contemporary jig composed by Scottish harpist and pianist Ingrid Henderson. Written in 6/8 meter and set in the key of A major, the tune features an engaging and rhythmic character well-suited to modern traditional ensemble playing. While it does not carry an older historical association, it has become a recognizable piece within the contemporary Celtic music repertoire.
