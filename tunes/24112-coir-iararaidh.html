Coir'-Iararaidh

Strathspey · 4/4 · D major

Coir'-Iararaidh is a traditional Scottish strathspey in four-four meter and D major composed by Bruce MacGregor. The tune is characterized by the dotted rhythms and lively character typical of traditional Scottish fiddle music. Because its precise historical associations are not well documented, its performance relies on the energetic and rhythmic playing style customary for contemporary interpretations of Highland strathspeys.
