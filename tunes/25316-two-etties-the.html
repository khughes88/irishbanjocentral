Two Etties, The

Reel · 4/4 · C major

The reel The Two Etties is a modern composition in 4/4 meter and C major written by the accomplished Irish fiddle player and composer Josephine Keegan. Featuring an upbeat and lively character, it is designed for energetic ensemble playing and is frequently performed in traditional Irish music sessions. Because the tune was recently composed, its historical associations are limited to its contemporary popularity within the traditional music community.
