Hanter Fisel

March · 4/4 · G major

Hanter Fisel is a traditional Breton dance tune from France, associated with the Fisel region and danced in a distinct three-step movement despite its standard meter. The precise origin and composer of the tune are not historically documented, so it is best known through generations of oral tradition within local fest-noz gatherings. Musically, it is set in G major and typically performed with a lively, driving character suited to accompanying traditional chain dances.
