Polka · 2/4 · D major

The polka known as The Trip To Dingle is a modern composition written by piano accordion player Alan Kelly. Set in the key of D major with a 2/4 meter, the tune is characterized by a lively and rhythmic feel typical of traditional Irish dance music. Although it originates from a contemporary artist rather than the older traditional repertoire, it has become widely adopted and played in sessions by contemporary musicians.
