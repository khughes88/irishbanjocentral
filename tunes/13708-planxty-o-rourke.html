Planxty O'Rourke

Jig · 6/8 · G major

Planxty O'Rourke is a traditional Irish jig composed in G major by the legendary blind harper Turlough O'Carolan. Written in 6/8 meter, the tune features the graceful and flowing character typical of O'Carolan's planxties, which were celebratory compositions dedicated to his patrons and notable figures of the era. It is commonly played with a lively yet lyrical feel on various traditional instruments, making it a well-established favorite in Irish traditional music sessions.
