Bellydancer, The

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

The march known as The Bellydancer was composed by the prominent Scottish bagpiper Gordon Duncan. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the A mixolydian mode, the tune reflects Duncan's innovative approach to contemporary pipe music. It is played with a lively, rhythmic drive that characterizes much of his modern traditional repertoire.
