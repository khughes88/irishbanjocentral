Falcarragh, The

Hornpipe · 4/4 · D major

The hornpipe The Falcarragh is a traditional-style tune composed by Alph Duggan and set in D major. It is written in 4/4 meter, which is standard for hornpipes, giving it the characteristic bouncy rhythm and deliberate tempo suited for traditional Irish step dancing. Because specific historical associations and regional origins beyond its composer are not firmly documented, the tune is best known through modern sessions and recordings featuring its lively melodic line.
