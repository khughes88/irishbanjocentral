Amhrán Na BhFiann

March · 4/4 · D major

Amhran na bhFiann is a traditional Irish march composed by Peader Kearney in 1907 with English lyrics by Ernest Blythe and later Irish lyrics by Liam O Rain. Written in 4/4 meter and the key of D major, the tune features a proud, stirring character suited to its status as the national anthem of Ireland since 1926. It gained deep historical association with the Irish nationalist movement after being frequently sung by volunteers during the 1916 Easter Rising.
