Trip To Dublin, The

Strathspey · 4/4 · A major

The strathspey The Trip To Dublin was composed by the renowned Cape Breton fiddler Jerry Holland. Written in A major and in 4/4 meter, the tune features the driving rhythm and characteristic dotted figures typical of traditional Scottish and Cape Breton fiddle music. While it does not carry an older historical association, it has become a popular modern addition to the Celtic fiddle repertoire.
