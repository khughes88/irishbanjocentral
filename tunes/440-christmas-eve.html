Christmas Eve

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Christmas Eve is a traditional Irish reel in 4/4 time and G major composed by the well-known Fiddler Tommy Coen from County Galway. The tune is characterized by its lively, driving rhythm and intricate phrasing that demands a nimble fingerwork style typical of East Galway fiddling. It has become a cherished standard in traditional Irish sessions worldwide, frequently recorded by prominent groups and solo artists.
