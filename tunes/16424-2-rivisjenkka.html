2-Rivisjenkka

March · 4/4 · E minor

Composed by Antti Järvelä, 2-Rivisjenkka is a traditional-style Finnish march written in the key of E minor with a steady 4/4 meter. The tune features the driving rhythm and upbeat character typical of Nordic folk dance music, designed to accompany energetic stepping.
