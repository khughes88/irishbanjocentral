Iniscealtra

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Iniscealtra is a traditional Irish reel composed by the renowned accordion player Paddy O'Brien. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of D major, the tune exemplifies the lively rhythm and melodic fluidity characteristic of traditional mid-twentieth-century Irish dance music. Its title refers to Inis Cealtra, the historic monastic island located on Lough Derg in County Clare.
