Lurgan Park

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Lurgan Park is a traditional Irish reel composed by Patrick Cummins in the 4/4 meter and A dorian mode. The tune is typically played with a lively and energetic character that suits the fast-paced nature of traditional sessions and social dancing.
