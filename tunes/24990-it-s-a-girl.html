It's A Girl

Strathspey · 4/4 · G major

It's A Girl is a contemporary strathspey composed by John McCusker and Ian Carr. Set in 4/4 meter and the key of G major, the tune features the distinctive dotted rhythms and driving character typical of traditional Scottish strathspeys. Because the piece is of modern origin, it does not carry older historical associations and is frequently performed in contemporary Celtic and folk music settings.
