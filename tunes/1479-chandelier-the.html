Chandelier, The

Reel · 4/4 · D major

The Chandelier is a contemporary reel in 4/4 time and D major composed by the renowned Irish-American fiddler Liz Carroll. The tune features an uplifting, driving character and is typically played with the rhythmic lift and expressive ornamentation characteristic of modern Irish fiddle music. Because it was recently composed by a living master, it has circulated widely among traditional players and sessions worldwide without older historical associations.
