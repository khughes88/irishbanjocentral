Ozark Rag

Reel · 4/4 · F major

Ozark Rag is a traditional American fiddle tune played as a lively 4/4 reel in the key of F major. Because its exact composer and early history remain undocumented, the tune is best known through the oral traditions of old-time fiddlers. It is characterized by a bright, upbeat tempo and a bouncing string-crossing style designed for energetic dancing.
