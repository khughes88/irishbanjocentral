Tyrkky

Polka · 2/4 · A major

Composed by Onni Rajaniemi, Tyrkky is a traditional-style Finnish polka written in 2/4 meter and the key of A major. The piece features the upbeat, rhythmic character typical of Nordic folk dance music, designed to drive lively partner dancing. Because specific historical associations and early performance contexts for the tune are not extensively documented, its enduring appeal rests entirely on its bright melody and traditional polka styling.
