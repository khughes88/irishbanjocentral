Junior Gill

Jig · 6/8 · E dorian

Junior Gill is a traditional Irish jig in the key of E dorian with a standard 6/8 meter. The tune was composed by the fiddle player John Daly, who was active in the musical traditions of County Clare. Because detailed historical associations for the piece are not widely documented, its performance relies on the lively, flowing style characteristic of traditional west of Ireland jig playing.
