Boules Et Guirlandes

Three-two · 3/2 · E dorian

Boules Et Guirlandes is a contemporary three-two tune composed by Gilles Le Bigot. Written in E dorian, the tune follows a 3/2 meter that gives it a distinctively driving and asymmetrical rhythmic pulse. It is commonly played in modern Breton and Celtic folk circles, showcasing the intricate picking and modal harmonies characteristic of Le Bigot's guitar style.
