Quiet Man, The

Strathspey · 4/4 · D minor

The traditional music tune The Quiet Man is a strathspey composed by Jim Sutherland. Written in four-four meter and set in D minor, it features the characteristically dotted rhythms and driving cadence of the Scottish strathspey style. Because specific historical associations and regional origins beyond the composer's attribution are not definitively documented, the tune is best defined by its distinctively rhythmic and expressive contemporary instrumental character.
