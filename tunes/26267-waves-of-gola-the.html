Waves Of Gola, The

March · 4/4 · D major

The Waves of Gola is a 4/4 march in D major composed by Mark Kelly. Because the broader traditional background and historical associations of the tune are not well documented, its identity rests on this specific modern composition. It is typically played with a steady, rhythmic bounce characteristic of traditional Irish marches, lending itself well to both solo and ensemble performance.
