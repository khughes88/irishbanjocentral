Washington Square Park

Three-two · 3/2 · E major

Washington Square Park is a modern three-two tune composed by Angus Lyon and Ruaridh Campbell. Written in E major with a 3/2 meter, it is performed with a steady, driving rhythm characteristic of contemporary Scottish traditional music. While it lacks deep historical associations due to its modern composition, the tune has become a popular addition to contemporary folk sessions.
