Strathspey · 4/4 · D major

Mr McAndrew's is a traditional Scottish strathspey composed by Donald Grant in the D major scale. Written in 4/4 meter, it features the characteristic dotted rhythms and snapped notes typical of the strathspey genre. This uplifting fiddle tune is designed for energetic dancing and is frequently featured in modern Scottish traditional music repertoires.
