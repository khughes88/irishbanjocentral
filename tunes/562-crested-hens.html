Crested Hens

Waltz · 3/4 · E dorian

Composed by French hurdy-gurdy player Gilles Chabenat, Crested Hens is a contemporary European folk waltz set in three-four meter and the E dorian mode. The tune is characterized by a graceful, flowing melody that blends traditional bourree sensibilities with modern folk-dance aesthetics. It is widely embraced in contemporary bal folk and European acoustic music circles as a popular repertoire staple for social dancing.
