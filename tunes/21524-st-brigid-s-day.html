St. Brigid's Day

Slip jig · 9/8 · G major

St. Brigid's Day is a contemporary slip jig composed in G major by concertina player and dancer Caitlín Nic Gabhann. Set in a lively 9/8 meter, the tune features an energetic and rhythmic character well-suited to both instrumental performance and traditional dance accompaniment. As a modern composition, it reflects the ongoing evolution of Irish traditional music without a historical association or traditional background.
