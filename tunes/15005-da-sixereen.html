Da Sixereen

Hornpipe · 4/4 · D major

Da Sixereen is a 4/4 hornpipe in D major composed by the renowned Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson. The tune is characterized by its lively rhythm and distinctly nautical Shetland style, reflecting the traditional six-rowed fishing boats it was named after. Because the historical record surrounding its exact composition date is uncertain, the tune is best defined by its enduring popularity within contemporary Shetland fiddle repertoires.
