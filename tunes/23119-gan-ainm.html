Gan Ainm

March · 4/4 · D major

Gan Ainm is a traditional Irish march composed by Tommy Kinsella in the key of D major. The tune is written in a standard 4/4 meter and exhibits a lively, rhythmic character typical of traditional instrumental dance music. Because detailed historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, the tune is best known through its continued performance in contemporary Irish session playing.
