Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Gan Ainm is an Irish traditional waltz in three-four time and the key of G major, with the title translating from Irish as "without name." Because the tune's specific geographic origin and exact historical background are unrecorded, it circulates anonymously within the traditional repertoire.
