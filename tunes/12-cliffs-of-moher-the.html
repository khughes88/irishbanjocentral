Cliffs Of Moher, The

Jig · 6/8 · D dorian

The Cliffs of Moher is a traditional Irish double jig set in six-eight time and usually played in the Dorian mode. Characterized by its lively, energetic movement and distinctive string of rolling triplets, it is a popular staple among traditional session musicians. Because its exact historical origins and early composer remain unrecorded, the tune is preserved purely through oral tradition and widespread regional performance.
