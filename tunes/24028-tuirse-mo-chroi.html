Tuirse Mo Chroí

Jig · 6/8 · A mixolydian

Tuirse Mo Chroí is a traditional Irish jig in six-eight meter and A mixolydian mode, though its exact geographic origin and composer remain unknown. The tune is characterized by its plaintive, weary melodic shape, matching the Irish title which translates as Weariness of My Heart.
