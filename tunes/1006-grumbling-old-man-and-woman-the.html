Grumbling Old Man And Woman, The

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

The Grumbling Old Man And Woman, The is a traditional fiddle tune of uncertain origin, widely preserved within Appalachian and old-time musical repertoires. As a lively reel in 4/4 meter and A Dorian mode, it is typically played with a driving, rhythmic accompaniment that suits energetic square dancing. The tune's contrasting melodic strains are designed to humorously evoke the distinct personalities and bickering of an old married couple.
