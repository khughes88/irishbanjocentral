Teribus

March · 4/4 · D major

Teribus is a traditional Scottish border march tune of uncertain age, serving as the anthem of the town of Hawick. Set in 4/4 time in the key of D major, it is played with a brisk, stirring character suited for ceremonial processions and pipe bands. Its most prominent historical association is with the annual Hawick Common Riding festival, where locals have sung and marched to it for generations.
