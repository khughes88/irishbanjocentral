Blackbird, The

Jig · 6/8 · D major

The Blackbird is a traditional Irish setting played as a jig in six-eight meter and typically performed in the key of D major. Because its exact composer and historical origin are not historically documented, musicians focus on its expressive, flowing character and ornamented regional playing styles. It is widely recognized as a classic cornerstone of the traditional Irish repertoire, cherished by both instrumentalists and set dancers alike.
