Lady Iveagh

Polka · 2/4 · A dorian

Lady Iveagh is a traditional Irish polka composed by the seventeenth-century harper and composer Thomas Connellan. Written in the key of A Dorian with a 2/4 meter, the tune features a lively and rhythmic character well-suited for traditional dance accompaniment. Because specific historical documentation regarding its exact composition date is uncertain, the piece is best appreciated through its enduring presence in the Irish folk repertoire.
