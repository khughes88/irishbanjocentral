Berceuse Pour Hannah

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Composed in D major by Richard Forest, Réjean Brunet, and Sabin Jacques, Berceuse Pour Hannah is a contemporary waltz in 3/4 meter. The piece features a gentle and expressive character, reflecting a soothing lullaby style well-suited for its dedication. Because it is a modern composition, it carries no traditional historical associations or older provenance.
