Lord Massereene

March · 4/4 · E minor

Lord Massereene is a 4/4 march in E minor composed by the legendary Irish harper Turlough O'Carolan. The tune exhibits a stately and dignified character typical of O'Carolan's instrumental compositions, blending traditional modal qualities with baroque influences. While specific historical associations with the Massereene title remain largely uncertain, the piece is well established in the repertoire as a fine example of the blind composer's enduring legacy.
