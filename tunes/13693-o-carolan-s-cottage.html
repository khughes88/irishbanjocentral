O'Carolan's Cottage

Waltz · 3/4 · A minor

O'Carolan's Cottage is a traditional waltz composed by the blind Irish harpist Turlough O'Carolan in the early eighteenth century. Written in 3/4 meter and the key of A minor, the tune features a graceful, flowing melody that reflects the lyrical style characteristic of its creator's surviving works.
