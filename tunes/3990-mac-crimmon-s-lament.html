Mac Crimmon's Lament

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Mac Crimmon's Lament is a traditional Scottish tune associated with the legendary piping family of Skye, though its exact historical origins and composition date remain uncertain. It is traditionally played with a slow, solemn, and expressive character that befits its mournful title, contrasting with the upbeat reel meter often associated with the name. While frequently connected in tradition to the Jacobite risings and the departure of pipers for war, these historical associations are largely romanticized and difficult to verify from primary sources.
