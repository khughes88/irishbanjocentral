Whycocomagh Bay

Reel · 4/4 · D mixolydian

Composed by the renowned Cape Breton fiddler Gordon MacQuarrie, Whycocomagh Bay is a lively reel set in 4/4 meter. The tune is written in the D mixolydian mode, giving it a characteristic modal flavor that drives its upbeat and energetic Scottish-Canadian dance rhythm. It is frequently performed in Cape Breton fiddle repertoires as a spirited showcase of traditional regional style.
