Ballyhoura Mountains, The

Polka · 2/4 · A major

The Ballyhoura Mountains, The is a traditional 2/4 polka in the key of A major composed by Terry Teahan. Composed by the accordion and fiddle player who emigrated from County Kerry to Chicago, it reflects the lively dance style of the Sliabh Luachra region. Specific historical associations and exact dates of composition remain unrecorded for this tune.
