Jig · 6/8 · D major

Eshaness Two-Step is a traditional-style jig composed by the prominent Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson. Written in 6/8 meter and the key of D major, the tune features the lively and rhythmic character typical of Shetland fiddle repertoire. Because detailed historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, the tune is best known through its presence in modern Shetland fiddle collections and sessions.
