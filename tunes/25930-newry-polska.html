Newry Polska

Waltz · 3/4 · A dorian

Newry Polska is a traditional tune whose exact origins and composer are not historically documented. Despite its name referencing a Northern Irish town, the piece adopts the distinctive 3/4 meter and character of a Scandinavian polska played with a steady, lilting dance rhythm. Because of its unusual blend of Celtic and Nordic musical traits, it is most commonly performed today in contemporary folk sessions and Scandinavian dance workshops.
