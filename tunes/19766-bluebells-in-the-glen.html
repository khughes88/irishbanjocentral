Bluebells In The Glen

Reel · 4/4 · G dorian

Bluebells In The Glen is a contemporary reel composed by Irish fiddler Yvonne Casey, set in the key of G dorian with a 4/4 meter. The tune features an uplifting, lyrical character typical of modern Clare composition while maintaining the steady, driving momentum required for traditional dance accompaniment. It is frequently performed in Irish traditional music sessions and has been recorded by contemporary artists within the genre.
