Long Note, The

Jig · 6/8 · D dorian

The Long Note is a traditional Irish double jig in 6/8 meter, typically played in the key of D Dorian. Its exact geographical origin and composer remain unknown, as the tune has been passed down primarily through oral tradition and archival recordings of traditional musicians.
