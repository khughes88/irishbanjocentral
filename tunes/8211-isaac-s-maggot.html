Isaac's Maggot

Three-two · 3/2 · D major

Isaac's Maggot is a traditional English country dance tune that first appeared in print in the seventh edition of John Playford's Dancing Master, published in 1686. Set in three-two meter and D major, it features the lively character and rhythmic drive typical of seventeenth-century English playford tunes. Because its specific composer is not recorded in historical sources, it remains classified strictly as a traditional instrumental piece from the late Stuart era.
