Glen Grant

Strathspey · 4/4 · A major

Glen Grant is a traditional Scottish strathspey in four-four meter and A major, though its exact origin and composer are not historically documented. As a strathspey, it features a brisk and driving rhythm characterized by the distinct dotted-note and cut-short pattern known as the Scotch snap. It is typically played with a spirited and rhythmic bowing style well-suited for traditional Scottish step dancing.
