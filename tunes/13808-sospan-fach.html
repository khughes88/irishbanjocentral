Sospan Fach

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

Sospan Fach is a traditional Welsh folk song and rugby anthem whose exact origins remain uncertain. The tune is typically performed in a lively, spirited manner with a steady march-like or duple rhythm suited for enthusiastic public singing. Over the decades, it has become famously associated with the Llanelli Scarlets rugby club and the industrial heritage of South Wales.
