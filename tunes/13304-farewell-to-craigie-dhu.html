Farewell To Craigie Dhu

Barndance · 4/4 · D mixolydian

Farewell To Craigie Dhu is a well-known contemporary Scottish barndance composed by renowned singer-songwriter Dougie MacLean. Written in 4/4 meter and typically played in the key of D mixolydian, the tune features an upbeat, lyrical, and distinctly driving character suited for traditional folk instrumentation. It has since become a cherished standard within modern Celtic and fiddle music circles, frequently performed at sessions and gatherings worldwide.
