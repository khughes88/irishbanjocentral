Every Good Boy Does Fine

Jig · 6/8 · A dorian

Every Good Boy Does Fine is a contemporary jig composed by Gian Marco Pietrasanta. Written in A dorian mode, the tune utilizes a lively 6/8 meter that drives its upbeat and rhythmic character. Because of its recent composition, it does not carry older historical associations and is primarily known through modern folk and acoustic settings.
