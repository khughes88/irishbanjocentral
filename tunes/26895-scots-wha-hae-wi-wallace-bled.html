Scots Wha Hae Wi' Wallace Bled

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

Scots Wha Hae Wi' Wallace Bled is a traditional Scottish march set to the ancient air Hey Tutti Taitie, with lyrics written by Robert Burns in 1793. The tune is written in 4/4 meter using the A mixolydian mode, delivering a stirring, defiant, and martial character well-suited to its role as a patriotic anthem. Although its precise earlier origins remain uncertain, the melody became historically associated with Scottish nationalism and the Battle of Bannockburn.
