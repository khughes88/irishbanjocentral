Dust

Three-two · 3/2 · E dorian

Dust is a contemporary three-two hornpipe composed by Irish flute player Brian Finnegan. Written in E dorian, the tune features a distinctive 3/2 meter that requires a bouncing and driving rhythmic feel typical of modern Irish traditional compositions. While it lacks deep historical associations due to its modern provenance, it has become a popular piece among contemporary players of Celtic music.
