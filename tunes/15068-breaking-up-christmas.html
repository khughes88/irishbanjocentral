Breaking Up Christmas

Reel · 4/4 · A major

Breaking Up Christmas is a traditional American fiddle tune associated with the old-time mountain music of the Upland South. Played in 4/4 time as a lively reel in A major, it features a driving, rhythmic style well suited for square dancing. Historical records and field collections link the tune primarily to traditional holiday festivities and community dances in regions like the Blue Ridge Mountains.
