Spotted Dog, The

Jig · 6/8 · A major

The Spotted Dog, The is a contemporary 6/8 jig composed by John McEvoy in the A major key. As a lively and rhythmic modern Irish dance tune, it is typically played with a driving, buoyant style characteristic of session music. Because it is a relatively recent addition to the traditional repertoire, it does not carry historical associations from earlier centuries, but it has become a popular choice among contemporary traditional musicians.
