Marcha De Brañes

March · 4/4 · A major

Marcha De Brañes is a traditional Asturian march composed by the renowned gaita player Xuacu Amieva. Set in 4/4 meter and the key of A major, the piece features the proud, rhythmic character typical of ceremonial bagpipe processional music. It serves as a modern contribution to the regional repertoire, frequently performed to accompany traditional Asturian festivities and cultural gatherings.
