Johnny D's

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

Johnny D's is a modern reel composed by renowned Irish-American fiddler Liz Carroll in the 4/4 meter. Set in the key of E dorian, the tune features an energetic, driving character well-suited for fast-paced traditional session playing. Because it is a contemporary composition rather than a centuries-old folk tune, it lacks older historical associations but is widely celebrated among modern fiddle players.
