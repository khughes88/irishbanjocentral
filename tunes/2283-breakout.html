Breakout

Jig · 6/8 · E dorian

Breakout is a modern jig composed by Ronan Hardiman for the theatrical show Lord of the Dance. Set in 6/8 meter and the key of E dorian, the tune features an energetic, driving rhythm characteristic of contemporary Celtic dance music. It is performed in a fast-paced, highly theatrical style designed to accompany rapid-fire hard shoe Irish dancing.
