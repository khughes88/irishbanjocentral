Garrett Barry's

Hornpipe · 4/4 · E dorian

Garrett Barry's is a traditional Irish hornpipe composed by the accordion player Jackie Daly and named after the renowned blind Clare piper Garrett Barry. Set in four-four meter and the key of E Dorian, the tune features the bouncy, dotted rhythms and bright character typical of traditional Irish hornpipes. Because exact historical details regarding its creation remain somewhat uncertain, the tune is primarily celebrated today as a vibrant staple of modern session repertoire.
