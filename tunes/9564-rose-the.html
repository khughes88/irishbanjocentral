Rose, The

Reel · 4/4 · F major

The Rose is a traditional Irish reel in four-four meter and F major that has circulated widely within the traditional session repertoire for generations. It is typically played with a lively, rhythmic drive characteristic of the genre, emphasizing crisp articulation and steady phrasing suitable for dancing. Because early printed and manuscript sources for the tune are fragmented and lack a definitive composer attribution, its exact geographic origin and precise historical associations remain uncertain.
