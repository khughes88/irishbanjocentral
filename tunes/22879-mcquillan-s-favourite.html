McQuillan's Favourite

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

McQuillan's Favourite is a contemporary reel in four-four time composed by Erin McGeown in the E minor mode. The tune features an upbeat and driving character typical of modern Irish dance music, making it well suited for lively session playing and fiddle accompaniment. Because it is a recently composed piece, it does not carry the deep historical associations of older traditional repertoire, but it has steadily gained popularity among contemporary musicians.
