Boston Blues

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Boston Blues is a contemporary reel composed by musician Cormac Breatnach. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of E minor, the tune blends traditional Irish instrumentation with contemporary jazz and blues influences. Because the piece is a modern composition rather than a centuries-old folk tune, it does not carry historical associations from earlier traditional repertoires.
