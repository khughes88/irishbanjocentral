Cheeky Robin, The

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

The Cheeky Robin is a 4/4 hornpipe in the key of G major composed by Barry Ruffell. As a modern contribution to the traditional hornpipe repertoire, it is characterized by a lively and rhythmic character well suited to standard playing styles. Specific historical associations and the exact date of composition remain undocumented, leaving the tune to be appreciated primarily through its engaging melodic structure and upbeat tempo.
