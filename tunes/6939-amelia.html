Amelia

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Amelia is a waltz composed in D major by the well-known American contradata composer Bob McQuillen. Written in 3/4 meter, the tune features a graceful and melodic character that makes it a popular choice in New England-style contra dance and folk music circles. Because its historical background beyond McQuillen's authorship is sparse, the piece is best appreciated through its flowing rhythm and appealing fiddle-tune structure.
