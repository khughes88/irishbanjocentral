Balmoral Highlanders, The

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

The 4/4 march The Balmoral Highlanders was composed by the prominent nineteenth-century piper and collector Angus MacKay. Written in the A Mixolydian mode, the tune exhibits the structured phrasing and steady rhythm characteristic of traditional Scottish pipe marches. It is closely associated with the rich heritage of Highland piping and the royal estate from which it takes its name.
