John Anderson My Jo

Reel · 4/4 · A minor

John Anderson My Jo is a traditional Scottish tune first recorded in the sixteenth century with lyrics that have since been adapted by various writers including Robert Burns. Set in A minor with a 4/4 meter and structured as a reel, the piece carries a lyrical and reflective character that lends itself to expressive traditional interpretation. While its exact origins remain uncertain, the melody gained widespread historical association through Burns's poignant late-eighteenth-century reworking of the song.
