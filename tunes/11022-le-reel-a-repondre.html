Le Reel A Repondre

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Le Reel A Repondre is a traditional French-Canadian reel played in four-four meter and the key of D major. Because its exact historical origin and composer are not historically documented, the tune is best known through its circulation in traditional fiddling repertoires. The music is characterized by a lively, driving rhythm and an upbeat character well-suited for traditional dance settings.
