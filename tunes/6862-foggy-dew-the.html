Foggy Dew, The

March · 4/4 · E minor

The Foggy Dew is a well-known traditional Irish tune of uncertain historical origin and no verified original composer. It is typically played as a 4/4 march in E minor, characterized by a spirited yet melancholy melodic line suited to traditional instrumental performance. Because its early history remains largely undocumented, musicians focus primarily on its distinctive modal character and rhythmic drive within contemporary folk settings.
