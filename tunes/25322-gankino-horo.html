Gankino Horo

March · 4/4 · C major

Gankino horo is a traditional Bulgarian dance tune arranged in C major by the renowned accordionist Boris Karlov. Though classified here as a march, the piece is typically associated with the lively asymmetric dance rhythms of the Thracian region. The style features nimble, highly ornamented accordion playing that drives a fast-paced and energetic musical character.
