Rosc Catha Na Mumhan

March · 4/4 · D major

Rosc Catha Na Mumhan, which translates to The Battle Cry of Munster, is a traditional Irish march played in 4/4 time within the key of D major. Because its exact historical origins and composer are not definitively documented, it is best known through its widespread performance in traditional Irish music circles. The tune is characterized by a strong, stirring rhythm and a bold character that suits the martial energy of a traditional parade or ceremonial march.
