I Dreamt I Dwelt In Marble Halls

Waltz · 3/4 · C dorian

I Dreamt I Dwelt In Marble Halls is a traditional waltz composed by Michael Balfe for his 1843 opera The Bohemian Girl. Written in 3/4 meter and C dorian, it is characterized by a flowing, graceful rhythm and a melancholy, lyrical vocal melody. The song achieved widespread popularity in the 19th century and remains a well-known standard in both classical and traditional repertoires.
