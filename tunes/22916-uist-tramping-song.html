Uist Tramping Song

March · 4/4 · G major

The Uist Tramping Song is a traditional Scottish marching tune associated with the island of North Uist in the Outer Hebrides. Composed in 4/4 meter and typically played in the key of G major, it features a steady, purposeful rhythm well-suited for walking and marching. Because specific historical details regarding its creation and early performance are not well documented, the tune is best known through its enduring presence in the traditional Gaelic and bagpipe repertoires.
