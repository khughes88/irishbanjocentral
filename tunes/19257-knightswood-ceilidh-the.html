Knightswood Ceilidh, The

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

The 4/4 march The Knightswood Ceilidh was composed by the prominent Scottish bagpipe writer and player Donald MacLeod. Written in A mixolydian, the tune features a lively and uplifting rhythmic character well suited for Scottish country dancing and modern pipe band performances. Because specific historical associations beyond its composition are not widely documented, its enduring popularity relies entirely on its engaging melodic structure and traditional ceilidh style.
