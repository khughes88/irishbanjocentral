Hounds Way, The

Reel · 4/4 · F major

The reel known as The Hounds Way is a contemporary composition in 4/4 meter written by musician Colin Farrell. Set in the key of F major, the tune features an energetic and driving character well-suited to standard Irish traditional dance sessions. Because it is a modern creation, it lacks older historical associations, but it has gained popularity among players of Celtic music for its engaging melody.
