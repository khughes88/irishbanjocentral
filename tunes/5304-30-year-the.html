30 Year, The

Jig · 6/8 · G major

The Thirty Year is a contemporary 6/8 jig composed by the Swedish guitarist Roger Tallroth of the band Väsen. Played in G major, the tune features an intricate, driving rhythm and a warm, modern folk character that showcases contemporary Scandinavian acoustic styling. While it lacks older historical associations due to its modern origin, it has become a well-regarded staple in contemporary folk repertoire among sessions and concert performers alike.
