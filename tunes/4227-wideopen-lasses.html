Wideopen Lasses

Three-two · 3/2 · G major

Wideopen Lasses is a contemporary three-two composed in G major by Northumbrian piper Chris Ormston. The tune is written in 3/2 meter and reflects the rhythmic drive characteristic of traditional border and Northumbrian music. Because of its modern origin, it does not carry historical associations from earlier centuries, but it has become a popular piece within the contemporary folk repertoire.
