John O'Connor

Jig · 6/8 · D major

John O'Connor is a traditional Irish jig in six-eight meter and D major composed by the blind harper Turlough O'Carolan during the early eighteenth century. The tune is characterized by a lively, graceful, and lyrical melody that reflects the characteristic style of the composer's blending of traditional Irish folk idioms with baroque musical influences. While specific historical documentation regarding the tune's namesake remains scarce, it is widely preserved and performed within both traditional Irish repertoire and historical harp collections.
