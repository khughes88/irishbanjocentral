Cucanandy

Slip jig · 9/8 · E minor

Cucanandy is a traditional Irish slip jig in nine-eight meter and the key of E minor, with its exact geographic origin and composer remaining unknown. It is characterized by its lively, bounding rhythm and cyclical melodic structure, making it a popular choice among traditional dancers and session musicians. Because historical documentation for the tune is scarce, it is best appreciated through its enduring presence in the oral folk music tradition.
