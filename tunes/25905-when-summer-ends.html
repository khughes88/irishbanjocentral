When Summer Ends

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

When Summer Ends is a contemporary waltz composed by the Scottish accordionist Phil Cunningham. Written in 3/4 meter and set in the key of D major, the tune is characterized by its lyrical melody and expressive, flowing style typical of modern Scottish folk composition. While it does not carry an older historical association due to its modern origin, it has become a well-regarded piece within the contemporary traditional music repertoire.
