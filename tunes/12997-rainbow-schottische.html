Rainbow Schottische

Barndance · 4/4 · D major

The Rainbow Schottische is a traditional barndance played in 4/4 meter with a bright and lively character suited for social dancing. Because its exact geographic origin and composer are not historically documented, it is best known through oral tradition and community fiddle repertoires. This upbeat D major tune features a steady, bouncing rhythm characteristic of traditional schottisches and barndances performed at folk dances.
