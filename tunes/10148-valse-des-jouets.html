Valse Des Jouets

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Valse Des Jouets is a contemporary waltz composed by the Quebecois musician and singer Michel Faubert. Written in 3/4 meter and typically played in the key of D major, the tune features a playful and whimsical character that reflects its French title, which translates to Waltz of the Toys. As a widely adopted piece in contemporary traditional folk circles, it is frequently featured in sessions and contra dance repertoires across North America.
