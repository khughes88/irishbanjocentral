Loch Mullardoch

Slip jig · 9/8 · A minor

Loch Mullardoch is a contemporary slip jig composed by Scottish fiddler Duncan Chisholm, set in 9/8 meter and A minor. The tune features a lyrical, evocative character that reflects the scenic beauty of the Scottish Highlands while demanding nimble fingerwork from performers. As a modern addition to the Celtic repertoire, it is frequently performed by contemporary fiddle players and folk ensembles.
