Hurricane, The

Reel · 4/4 · A major

The reel known as The Hurricane was composed by the renowned Scottish fiddler James Scott Skinner. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of A major, the tune is characterized by a high-energy, virtuosic style that demands advanced technical skill and precision. Although specific historical associations beyond its creation by Skinner are not widely documented, it remains a celebrated showcase piece within the Scottish traditional fiddle repertoire.
