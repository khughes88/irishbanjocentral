Jamie Come Try Me

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Jamie Come Try Me is a traditional Scottish tune published in the late eighteenth century, most notably appearing in the fourth volume of James Johnson's Scots Musical Instrument Museum in 1792 with lyrics associated with Robert Burns. Written in G major in 3/4 meter, the melody follows the graceful and flowing waltz form common to many fiddle and dance collections of the period. Because its specific earlier origins and composer are not historically documented, the tune is best known today through its enduring presence in traditional Scottish session repertoires.
