Marcha Processional Du Corpus De Pontevedra

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Marcha Processional Du Corpus De Pontevedra is a traditional Galician processional melody associated with Corpus Christi celebrations in Pontevedra, Spain. The tune is set in G major with a 3/4 meter and follows a waltz form, though its exact historical origins and authorship remain largely undocumented. Its stately character and flowing triple meter lend themselves to measured outdoor processionals rather than social dancing.
