Reel · 4/4 · D major

John McGrath's is a lively 4/4 reel in the key of D major composed by John McGrath. Because detailed historical records regarding its exact composition date and early regional transmission are scarce, the tune is best known today through its modern performance in traditional Irish music sessions.
