Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

Gan Ainm is an Irish hornpipe in 4/4 time and G major, with its specific composer and historical origins remaining unrecorded. Because the title translates from Irish Gaelic as nameless tune, musicians often use it as a generic identifier for anonymous pieces in the traditional repertoire.
