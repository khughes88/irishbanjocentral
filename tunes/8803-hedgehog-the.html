Hedgehog, The

Slip jig · 9/8 · D major

The Hedgehog is a contemporary slip jig in nine-eight meter and D major composed by the renowned Irish musician Vincent Broderick. Written in a lively and distinctive style characteristic of its composer's prolific output, the tune remains a popular addition to modern traditional Irish session repertoires. Because it is a twentieth-century composition by a known artist, it does not carry the anonymous historical associations of older traditional repertoire.
