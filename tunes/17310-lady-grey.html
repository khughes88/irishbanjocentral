Lady Grey is a contemporary three-two composed by English diatonic accordion player Andy Cutting. Written in E minor, the tune features the distinctive, driving 3/2 meter characteristic of traditional English border and regional dance music. It is frequently performed in folk settings to accompany energetic couple and step dances with a precise and lively rhythm.
