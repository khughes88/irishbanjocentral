Wagtail, The

Reel · 4/4 · D major

The Wagtail is a traditional Irish reel played in 4/4 meter with a lively, energetic character typical of standard session music. Because the composer and specific historical origins of the tune are not historically documented, it is primarily known through the oral tradition of traditional Irish musicians.
