Gan Ainm

Reel · 4/4 · A major

Gan Ainm is an Irish reel in four-four meter and A major whose exact origin and composer are not historically documented. Played in a lively and rhythmic style characteristic of traditional Irish dance music, this tune features a bright and energetic melodic contour well-suited for social dancing. Like many traditional melodies bearing this title, which translates from the Irish for without a name, it has been preserved and passed down through generations of tradition bearers.
