Rod Alexander's

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Rod Alexander's is a lively 4/4 reel in the key of A Dorian composed by the renowned Cape Breton fiddler Father Angus Morris. The tune is characterized by its driving traditional Scottish-derived rhythm and infectious energy, making it a popular choice in modern Cape Breton fiddle sessions. Because the history of many regional fiddle tunes remains undocumented beyond the composer's immediate circle, specific historical associations outside of its creation by Father Morris are not well established.
