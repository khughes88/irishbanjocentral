Cahan O'Hara

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Cahan O'Hara is a traditional waltz composed in 3/4 meter by the blind Irish harpist Turlough O'Carolan during the late seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries. Set in G major, the tune exhibits the graceful, lyrical, and flowing character typical of O'Carolan's distinctive compositional style. Because specific historical associations beyond its authorship are not firmly established, the melody is primarily appreciated for its enduring appeal within the traditional Irish repertoire.
