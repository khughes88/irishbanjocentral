Housegarth Two-Step

Jig · 6/8 · F major

Housegarth Two-Step is a contemporary jig composed by Jackie Linklater in the key of F major. The tune is structured in 6/8 meter, giving it a lively and rhythmic feel characteristic of traditional Shetland dance music. Because it is a modern composition by a known regional accordionist, it lacks older historical associations and is primarily played within contemporary folk music circles.
