Warbling Robin, The

Barndance · 4/4 · G major

The barndance known as The Warbling Robin was composed by the contemporary accordion player Marcus Hernon. Set in G major and 4/4 meter, the tune features an upbeat and lively character well-suited to traditional Irish instrumental performance. Because it is a modern composition by a known living tradition-bearer, it lacks the obscure origins and historical associations of older, anonymous folk tunes.
