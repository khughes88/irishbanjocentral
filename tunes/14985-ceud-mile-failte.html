Ceud Mile Failte

March · 4/4 · A major

Ceud Mile Failte is a four-four march in A major composed by John M. Mason. Because specific historical associations and exact geographic origins for the tune are not well documented, its background remains traditional in character. The piece is typically played in a steady, upright march style that suits its welcoming Gaelic title, which translates to A Hundred Thousand Welcomes.
