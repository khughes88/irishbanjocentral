Twelve Pins, The

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Composed by the renowned fiddler and pianist Charlie Lennon, The Twelve Pins is a contemporary Irish reel set in 4/4 time and G major. The tune exhibits the vibrant, driving rhythm characteristic of modern traditional Irish dance music and is frequently featured in session repertoires. It is named after the Twelve Bens mountain range in Connemara, reflecting Lennon's deep musical roots in County Leitrim and the west of Ireland.
