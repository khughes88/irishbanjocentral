Loch Maree

Waltz · 3/4 · A major

Loch Maree is a waltz composed in A major by the noted Scottish piper George S. MacLennan, though the exact date of its composition remains uncertain. Written in 3/4 meter, the tune features a graceful, flowing character typical of Scottish dance music and is traditionally performed with a lyrical and expressive playing style. It is widely played by fiddle and pipe enthusiasts who appreciate its melodic charm and connection to the Scottish Highland tradition.
