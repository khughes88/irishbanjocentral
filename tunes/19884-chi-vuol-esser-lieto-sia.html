Chi Vuol Esser Lieto Sia

Slip jig · 9/8 · G major

Chi Vuol Esser Lieto Sia is a traditional tune composed by Gian Marco Pietrasanta. It is structured as a slip jig in nine-eight meter and set in the key of G major.
