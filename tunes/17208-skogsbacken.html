Skogsbäcken

Waltz · 3/4 · C major

Skogsbäcken is a Swedish waltz composed by Lars Fredriksson in the three-quarter meter typical of the genre. Written in C major, the tune has a flowing character that reflects its woodland brook namesake. While specific historical associations and regional origins are not well documented, it remains a recognized piece within the Swedish traditional and folk repertoire.
