Merry Maids Polka, The

Barndance · 4/4 · G major

The Merry Maids Polka is a traditional-style barndance composed by Adam Rennie in G major. Written in 4/4 meter, the tune is characterized by a lively, rhythmic bounce well-suited for social dancing and traditional Scottish country music performance. Specific historical associations and the exact date of composition remain undocumented, allowing the music itself to stand on its established popularity among accordion and dance enthusiasts.
