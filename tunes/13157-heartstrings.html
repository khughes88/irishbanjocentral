Heartstrings

Waltz · 3/4 · C major

Heartstrings is a waltz composed in C major by concertina player and dancer Caitlín Nic Gabhann. Written in 3/4 meter, the tune features a melodic and expressive character well-suited to the fluid movement of the traditional waltz. Because its history and associations are otherwise unrecorded, the piece is best known through contemporary performances in Irish traditional music circles.
