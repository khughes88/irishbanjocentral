Flower Of Kristiansand, The

Waltz · 3/4 · A major

The tune The Flower Of Kristiansand is a contemporary waltz composed by Ivan Drever. Written in three-four meter and set in the key of A major, it features the graceful, flowing melodic style characteristic of modern folk waltzes. Because the tune is a modern composition by Drever rather than an older folk piece, it lacks traditional historical associations, serving instead as a popular showcase for expressive ensemble and solo playing.
