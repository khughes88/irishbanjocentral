Funk The Cajun Blues

Reel · 4/4 · A minor

Composed by fiddle player Gerry O'Connor, Funk The Cajun Blues is a contemporary reel set in the key of A minor with a 4/4 meter. The tune blends traditional Irish fiddle techniques with modern rhythmic and stylistic influences, creating an energetic and distinctive character. While it does not carry an older historical association due to its modern composition, it has become a popular piece within contemporary Celtic music repertoire.
