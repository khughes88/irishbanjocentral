Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

1st August is a contemporary reel composed by guitarist and bouzouki player Donogh Hennessy. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of E dorian, the tune features an energetic and driving rhythmic character well suited for modern Irish traditional sessions. Because it is a recently composed piece, it lacks the older historical associations of traditional tunes, instead gaining popularity through contemporary folk and instrumental recordings.
