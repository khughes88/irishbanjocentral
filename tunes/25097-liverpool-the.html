Liverpool, The

Reel · 4/4 · G major

The reel known as The Liverpool is a well-known traditional-style tune in 4/4 meter that is set in the key of G major. The composition of this specific modern setting is attributed to the well-known Irish fiddler and composer Charlie Lennon. It is typically played in a lively and rhythmic Irish traditional style that makes it popular in sessions and social dancing.
