Grind Hans Jässpôdspolska

Slip jig · 9/8 · G major

Grind Hans Jässpôdspolska is a traditional Swedish fiddle tune from Boda in Dalarna, associated with the fiddler Hans Jässpôd. It is performed in a lively 9/8 meter typical of traditional polskor, featuring a distinctive bouncing rhythm and rustic melodic ornamentation. Because the precise historical origins and composition date of the tune are uncertain, its character is defined primarily by the regional performance style of the Dalarna tradition.
