Northern Lights Of Old Aberdeen

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

The Northern Lights of Old Aberdeen is a popular Scottish song and waltz whose exact traditional origins and original composer are not historically verified. Set in three-four meter and usually played in the key of D major, the tune features a graceful, flowing tempo that supports sentimental and nostalgic vocal performances. It is widely recognized as a regional anthem closely associated with the city of Aberdeen and the broader culture of northeast Scotland.
