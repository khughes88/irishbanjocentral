An Saighdiúir Tréighthe

Waltz · 3/4 · C major

An Saighdiúir Tréighthe is a traditional Irish waltz, which translates from the Gaelic as The Forsaken Soldier. Set in three-four meter and the key of C major, it features a graceful, flowing rhythm well-suited for slow and expressive dancing. Because its exact composer and early history are not definitively documented, the tune's background remains largely traditional and rooted in community transmission.
