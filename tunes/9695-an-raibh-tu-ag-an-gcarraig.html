An Raibh Tú Ag An gCarraig?

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

An Raibh Tú Ag An gCarraig is a traditional Irish air with origins that are historically uncertain, though it is widely associated with the Gaelic poetry tradition. Typically performed as a waltz in three-four meter and set in E minor, the tune features a haunting and expressive character well suited for slow, contemplative playing styles. Because its precise historical beginnings and early associations remain largely unrecorded, the piece is best known through its enduring presence in traditional Irish vocal and instrumental repertoires.
