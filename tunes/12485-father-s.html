Father's

Jig · 6/8 · A dorian

Composed by the renowned Newfoundland fiddler Rufus Guinchard, Father's is a lively jig set in the key of A dorian. The tune features the characteristic 6/8 meter common to traditional Irish-influenced fiddle music of the region, driven by an energetic rhythm well-suited for dancing. Guinchard's distinct style has helped preserve this piece as a treasured part of the traditional Anglo-Acadian and Newfoundland repertoire.
