Childgrove

Barndance · 4/4 · A minor

Childgrove is an English country dance tune first published in the 10th edition of John Playford's The Dancing Master in 1998, though its precise earlier origins remain uncertain. The tune features a lively character typically set in four-four meter and A minor, making it well-suited for spirited traditional social dancing. Because historical records regarding its exact composition are lacking, it is best known through its enduring presence in the revival of seventeenth- and eighteenth-century English dance repertoires.
