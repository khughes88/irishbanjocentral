Reel · 4/4 · A mixolydian

Pressed for Time is a modern reel composed by the renowned Scottish piper Gordon Duncan. Written in 4/4 meter and set in A mixolydian, the tune features the driving rhythm, technical brilliance, and contemporary edge characteristic of Duncan's best work.
