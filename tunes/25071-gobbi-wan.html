Gobbi Wan

Reel · 4/4 · A mixolydian

Gobbi Wan is a contemporary reel composed by Scottish musician Ross Ainslie. Written in 4/4 meter, the tune is set in the key of A Mixolydian. Because it is a modern composition rather than an older folk tune, it does not carry a traditional background or historical association.
