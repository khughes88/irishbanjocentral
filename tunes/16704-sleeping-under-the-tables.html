Sleeping Under The Tables

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Sleeping Under The Tables is a contemporary reel composed by Nolan Ladewski in the A Dorian mode with a 4/4 meter. The tune is driven by a lively, rhythmic character typical of modern fiddle music and is designed with an upbeat playing style well-suited for sessions. Because of its recent origin, the piece does not have older historical associations, focusing instead on contemporary instrumental creativity.
