Lament For Terrence MacDonough

Waltz · 3/4 · A minor

Lament For Terrence MacDonough is a traditional Irish waltz composed in the early eighteenth century by the blind harper Turlough O'Carolan. Set in a 3/4 meter and the key of A minor, the tune is characterized by a graceful yet deeply melancholy melody that reflects the expressive style of the late Gaelic harp tradition. The piece was written to commemorate Terrence MacDonough, a prominent seventeenth-century Irish lawyer and member of the MacDonough clan of County Sligo.
