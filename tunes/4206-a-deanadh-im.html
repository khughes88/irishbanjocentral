A' Dèanadh Ìm

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

A' Dèanadh Ìm is a traditional Scottish Gaelic reel in 4/4 time with no verified composer of record. Set in the A dorian mode, the tune is traditionally associated with the rhythmic motions of butter-making, as reflected in its Gaelic title. Because its exact historical origins and early documentation remain uncertain, its musical character is defined by a lively and rhythmic style suited for traditional dancing.
