Caha Mountains

Reel · 4/4 · D mixolydian

Caha Mountains is a traditional Irish reel composed by fiddle player John Dwyer. Set in 4/4 time and the key of D Mixolydian, the tune features an energetic and driving character well-suited for lively sessions. Because its history outside of Dwyer's authorship is largely undocumented, players focus on its distinctive modal melody and rhythmic momentum.
