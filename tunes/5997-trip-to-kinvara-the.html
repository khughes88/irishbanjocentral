Trip To Kinvara, The

Reel · 4/4 · D major

The reel known as The Trip to Kinvara is a well-crafted traditional Irish composition by the renowned accordion player and fiddler Martin Mulhaire. Set in four-four meter and the key of D major, the tune is characterized by a lively and rhythmic style that makes it popular in traditional sessions. While specific historical associations are not widely documented, Mulhaire's legacy as a member of the Tulla Céilí Band underpins the established pedigree of his compositions within East Galway musical traditions.
