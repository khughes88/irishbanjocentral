Galway Races

Jig · 6/8 · A minor

Galway Races is a traditional Irish jig in 6/8 meter and A minor, though its exact origin and composer are not historically established. The tune features an energetic, driving character that makes it exceptionally popular for energetic social dancing and lively instrumental sessions. It is widely associated with the famous annual horse-racing festival in County Galway, reflecting the vibrant atmosphere of that traditional gathering.
