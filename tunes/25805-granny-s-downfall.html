Granny's Downfall

Jig · 6/8 · A major

Granny's Downfall is a modern Irish jig composed by the accordion player Conal Hernon. Written in six-eight meter and the key of A major, it is an upbeat and lively instrumental piece frequently featured in traditional music sessions. Because it is a contemporary composition, it lacks the deep historical associations of older folk tunes, but it has gained popularity among modern players for its driving rhythm and melodic energy.
