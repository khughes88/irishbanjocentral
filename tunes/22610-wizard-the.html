Wizard, The

Jig · 6/8 · A minor

The Wizard is a contemporary jig in six-eight meter and A minor composed by Scottish fiddler Adam Sutherland. Driven by a lively and modern melodic character, it is frequently performed in contemporary Celtic and Scottish folk sessions with dynamic ornamentation and energetic bowing. While its exact date of composition is uncertain, the tune has quickly become a popular staple in the modern folk repertoire among both soloists and ensemble players.
