Farewell To Jim

Jig · 6/8 · D major

Farewell To Jim is a contemporary Irish jig composed by the well-known accordion player and piano accompanist Carl Hession. Written in six-eight meter and set in the key of D major, the tune exemplifies the lively and melodic style characteristic of modern Irish traditional compositions. Because it is a modern creation, it does not carry historical associations from earlier traditional eras, but it has become a popular addition to sessions among contemporary musicians.
