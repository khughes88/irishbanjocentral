Gan Ainm

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

Gan Ainm is an Irish hornpipe in 4/4 time and G major, with the title translating from the Irish Gaelic for nameless. Because no specific composer is recorded, the tune circulates widely as an anonymous traditional piece within the Irish repertoire. It features the characteristic dotted rhythms and lively, bouncing character typical of traditional hornpipes played in sessions.
