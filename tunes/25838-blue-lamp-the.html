Blue Lamp, The

Reel · 4/4 · A major

The contemporary reel The Blue Lamp was composed in A major by Scottish fiddlers Gavin Marwick and Jonny Hardie. Written in standard 4/4 meter, the tune is characterized by an energetic and driving modern Scottish style suited for contra dance and session playing. While the exact date of its composition is not widely documented, it has become a popular addition to contemporary folk repertoires through recordings by artists like Malinky.
