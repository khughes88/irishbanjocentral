Deirdre

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Composed by Marnix Polfliet, Deirdre is a contemporary waltz written in three-four meter and set in the key of E minor. The piece features a flowing, graceful melodic character well-suited to the smooth phrasing typical of traditional European waltz styles. Because it is a modern composition rather than an older folk tune, it lacks the traditional historical associations or anonymous origins often found in older fiddle repertoire.
