Boys Of Ballivanich, The

Reel · 4/4 · B minor

The reel The Boys Of Ballivanich was composed by Ian Kirkpatrick in 4/4 meter and is set in the key of B minor. Because the wider traditional background and historical associations of the tune are not extensively documented, its character is best defined by its lively tempo and driving rhythmic structure typical of Scottish fiddle music. It is frequently performed by modern folk instrumentalists and accordion players as part of contemporary Celtic repertoire sets.
