Caoineadh An tSagairt

Waltz · 3/4 · A dorian

Caoineadh An tSagairt is an Irish traditional tune whose exact origin and composer are not historically documented. Set in A Dorian with a 3/4 meter, it is performed as a waltz with a mournful, reflective, and expressive musical character. The Gaelic title translates to The Priest's Lament, suggesting a connection to themes of ecclesiastical mourning or historical lamentation common in the tradition.
