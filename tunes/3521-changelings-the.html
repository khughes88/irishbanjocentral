Changelings, The

Waltz · 3/4 · B minor

The waltz The Changelings is a contemporary composition in 3/4 meter by Irish flute and tin whistle player Emer Mayock. Set in the key of B minor, the tune features a graceful yet haunting melodic character typical of modern Irish slow and waltz repertoire. Because its precise historical associations are not widely documented, it is primarily appreciated and performed today as a distinct showcase for Mayock's expressive compositional style.
