Säkkijärven Polkka

Polka · 2/4 · E minor

Sakkijarven Polkka is a traditional Finnish polka tune whose exact composer is not verified. Played in two-four meter and the key of E minor, the piece is characterized by its exceptionally fast tempo, lively rhythm, and virtuosic accordion style. During the Continuation War, the Finnish military famously used the tune to continuously jam Soviet radio-controlled landmines, cementing its status as a piece of wartime folklore.
