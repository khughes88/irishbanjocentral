Seven Years

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Seven Years is a contemporary waltz composed by the renowned English diatonic accordionist Andy Cutting. Written in 3/4 meter and set in the key of G major, the tune is characterized by its lyrical flow and emotive, contemporary folk sensibility. It is widely performed in European folk music circles, particularly by contemporary acoustic and roots ensembles.
