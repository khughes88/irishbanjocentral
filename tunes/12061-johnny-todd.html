Johnny Todd

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

Johnny Todd is a traditional British hornpipe tune of uncertain origin that has been known since at least the nineteenth century. Played in four-four meter and the key of G major, it features a lively and bouncy rhythm characteristic of traditional dance music. The melody is famously associated with the seaport of Liverpool and was later adopted as the theme song for the television series Z-Cars.
