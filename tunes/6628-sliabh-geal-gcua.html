Sliabh Geal gCua

Hornpipe · 4/4 · D major

Sliabh Geal gCua is a traditional Irish hornpipe composed by Pádraig Ó Mileadha, taking its name from a well-known mountain in County Waterford. Written in 4/4 meter and typically played in the key of D major, the tune features the steady, punctuated rhythm and measured pacing characteristic of traditional hornpipes. Because detailed historical records regarding its earliest performances remain scarce, the piece is best appreciated through its enduring presence in the regional instrumental repertoire of Munster.
