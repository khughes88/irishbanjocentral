Merry Go Round

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Merry Go Round is a lively reel in 4/4 time and G major composed by the prominent Irish button accordionist Sharon Shannon. The tune is characterized by its bright, upbeat melody and bouncy tempo, which are well-suited for energetic contra dancing and traditional Irish sessions. Shannon's original recording helped popularize the piece within contemporary folk and Celtic music repertoires.
