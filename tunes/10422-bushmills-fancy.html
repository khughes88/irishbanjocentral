Bushmills Fancy

Hornpipe · 4/4 · E minor

Bushmills Fancy is an Irish hornpipe composed by Paddy Mills in the E minor key and set in 4/4 meter. The tune is played with the characteristic upbeat tempo and swung rhythm typical of traditional hornpipes, making it well-suited for solo fiddle or group sessions. While specific historical associations or detailed regional origins beyond its connection to the composer are not widely documented, it remains a lively and enduring addition to the traditional repertoire.
