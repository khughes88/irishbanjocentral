Calum Of Benbecula

Strathspey · 4/4 · D major

Calum Of Benbecula is a contemporary strathspey composed by Fred Morrison in the key of D major. Written in 4/4 meter, the tune is designed with the characteristic dotted rhythms and driving cadence typical of Scottish highland strathspeys. Because it is a modern composition by a well-known master piper, it is frequently featured in contemporary bagpipe and traditional fiddle repertoires.
