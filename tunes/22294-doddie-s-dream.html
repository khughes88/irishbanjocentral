Doddie's Dream

March · 4/4 · G major

Doddie's Dream is a modern 4/4 march composed in G major by Scottish fiddler Bruce MacGregor. Written to honor the inspirational rugby union player and motor neurone disease campaigner Doddie Weir, the piece carries an uplifting and purposeful character suited to its march classification. The tune is typically performed in a spirited and driving style that reflects both the vitality of contemporary Scottish folk music and a strong sense of tribute.
