Poon Hill

Reel · 4/4 · B minor

Poon Hill is a contemporary reel in four-four time composed by flute and whistle player Brian Finnegan. Set in the key of B minor, the tune reflects modern stylistic developments in Irish traditional music while maintaining the driving rhythm characteristic of standard reels. Because its recent composition history is well documented, it lacks the older historical associations of traditional folk tunes.
