Lastringe Storpolska

Mazurka · 3/4 · D major

Lastringe Storpolska is a traditional Swedish storpolska from the parish of Lästringe in Södermanland, with no single historical composer recorded. Set in 3/4 time and the key of D major, this dance tune features the driving rhythm, asymmetric phrasing, and robust character typical of the regional polska tradition. Because detailed historical associations and specific composition dates for the tune are not well documented, it is preserved primarily through the oral folk music tradition of Uppland and Södermanland fiddle players.
