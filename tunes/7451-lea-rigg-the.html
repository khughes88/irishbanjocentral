March · 4/4 · G major

The traditional Scottish tune The Lea Rigg is a gentle air typically performed in a moderate 4/4 meter within the key of G major. While its exact composer and early origins remain uncertain, the melody gained widespread popularity after Robert Burns wrote new lyrics for it in the late eighteenth century. Today, it is frequently played as a steady march or lyrical song setting that highlights the pastoral charm of its traditional Scottish musical character.
