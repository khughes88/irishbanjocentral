Cullen Bay

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

Cullen Bay is a contemporary 4/4 march composed by Ian Duncan in the traditional Scottish style. Written in the key of A Mixolydian, the tune features a bright, driving rhythm well-suited for piping and marching. Because it is a modern composition, it does not carry older historical associations, but it has become a popular addition to modern bagpipe repertoires.
