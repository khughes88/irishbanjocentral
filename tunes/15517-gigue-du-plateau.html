Gigue Du Plateau

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Gigue Du Plateau is a modern reel composed by Jean Claude Belanger in the traditional style of French-Canadian fiddle music. Written in D major and set in 4/4 meter, it features an upbeat and driving rhythm designed to accompany lively step dancing.
