Reel Around The Sun

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Reel Around The Sun is a traditional-style Irish reel composed by Bill Whelan as the opening number for the 1994 theatrical show Riverdance. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of E minor, the piece features a driving, energetic rhythm that showcases rapid instrumental execution and modern orchestral arrangement. It achieved widespread international prominence and established a new standard for theatrical Irish dance music following its debut during the Eurovision Song Contest interval act.
