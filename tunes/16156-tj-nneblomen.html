Tjønneblomen

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Tjønneblomen is a traditional Norwegian waltz composed by hardanger fiddler Germund Haugen. Written in 3/4 meter and set in E minor, the tune is characterized by its lyrical melody and expressive folk dance rhythm. It is widely recognized within the hardanger fiddle tradition of Telemark, where Haugen was a prominent musical figure.
