Cullen Skink

Reel · 4/4 · C major

Cullen Skink is a traditional Scottish reel in four-four time and C major, sharing its name with the famous smoked haddock and potato soup from the town of Cullen in Moray. Because its precise historical origins and composer are not documented, musicians typically perform it with a lively, rhythmic drive characteristic of upbeat fiddle tunes. The melody features bright, energetic phrases well-suited to accompanying fast-paced Scottish country dancing.
