Jig Of Life

Jig · 6/8 · A dorian

Jig of Life is a contemporary jig in six-eight meter and A Dorian mode composed by the Irish musician and singer Sharon Shannon. The tune features an upbeat, driving character and is typically played with a lively rhythm that showcases modern Irish traditional stylistic influences. Because of its relatively recent composition, it does not possess the older historical associations of traditional folk tunes, but it has become a popular and widely recorded piece within the contemporary Celtic music repertoire.
