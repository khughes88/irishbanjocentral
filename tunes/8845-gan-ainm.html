Gan Ainm

Barndance · 4/4 · G major

Gan Ainm is an Irish barndance in 4/4 time and G major, with the title translating from Irish as nameless. Because the tune's specific composer and exact historical origins are not recorded, it survives purely through the traditional oral session repertoire. It is typically played with a bright, steady rhythm and a lively character well-suited for traditional social dancing.
