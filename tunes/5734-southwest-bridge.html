Southwest Bridge

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Southwest Bridge is a modern reel composed in 4/4 time and E minor by the renowned Cape Breton fiddler Dan R. MacDonald. The tune is characterized by its lively, driving rhythm and intricate phrasing that demands nimble bowing and precise fingering.
