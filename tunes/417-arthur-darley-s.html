Arthur Darley's

Jig · 6/8 · A mixolydian

Arthur Darley's is a traditional Irish jig composed in 6/8 meter by the prominent late 19th and early 20th-century fiddler Arthur Darley. The tune is set in the key of A Mixolydian, giving it a bright, modal character that is well-suited to spirited traditional fiddling. Because specific historical associations beyond its composition are not widely documented, it remains primarily celebrated within traditional repertoire as a showcase of Darley's melodic craftsmanship.
