Molly MacAlpin

Barndance · 4/4 · E minor

Molly MacAlpin is a traditional Irish tune composed by the 17th-century harpist William Connellan. Written in four-four meter and the key of E minor, it is typically performed as a lively barndance with an energetic and rhythmic character. Because specific historical associations beyond Connellan's authorship are largely unrecorded, the piece is best appreciated for its enduring melodic charm and traditional playing style.
