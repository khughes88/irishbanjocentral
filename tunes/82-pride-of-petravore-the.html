Pride Of Petravore, The

Hornpipe · 4/4 · E minor

The Pride of Petravore is a well-known Irish hornpipe composed by William Houston Collisson. Set in four-four meter and the key of E minor, the tune features a lively and bouncy rhythm characteristic of traditional dance music. It is frequently performed at Irish set dancing events and remains a popular staple among traditional instrumentalists today.
