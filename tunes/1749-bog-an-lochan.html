Bog An Lochan

Strathspey · 4/4 · E dorian

Bog An Lochan is an E dorian strathspey in 4/4 meter composed by Alexander McGlashan. As a traditional Scottish fiddle tune in the strathspey genre, it features the characteristic dotted rhythms and snapped accents associated with Highland dance music. The precise historical circumstances of its composition remain uncertain, but McGlashan included it in his 18th-century collection of reels and strathspeys.
