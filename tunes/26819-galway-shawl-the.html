Galway Shawl, The

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

The Galway Shawl is a well-known traditional Irish folk song and waltz set in three-quarter time and the key of G major. Its precise historical origin and composer are not definitively established, leaving the tune best known through generations of oral transmission and popular performance. The melody is typically performed with a smooth, flowing character that complements its graceful waltz rhythm and lyrical themes of romance along the road to Athenry.
