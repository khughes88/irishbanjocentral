Ar Mhuin Na Muice

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Ar Mhuin Na Muice is a well-known Irish reel composed by the esteemed fiddler and accordion player Seán Ryan. Set in the key of A Dorian with a 4/4 meter, the tune is characterized by its lively, traditional rhythmic drive and melodic ingenuity. It is frequently performed by contemporary traditional musicians in both solo settings and lively session environments.
