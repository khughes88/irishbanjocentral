I'll Learn It By Tomorrow

Slip jig · 9/8 · G major

Composed by Stephen Carolan, I'll Learn It By Tomorrow is a contemporary slip jig in the traditional Irish style. Written in G major with a lively 9/8 meter, the tune features the characteristic bouncing rhythm and flowing phrasing typical of the slip jig form. Because it is a recent composition by a known piper, it does not carry the centuries-old historical associations of older traditional tunes, but it has become a popular addition to modern session repertoires.
