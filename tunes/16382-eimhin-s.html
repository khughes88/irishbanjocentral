Eimhin's

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Composed by master uilleann piper Paddy Keenan, Eimhin's is a contemporary waltz set in three-four time and the key of E minor. The tune possesses a graceful, flowing character that suits the expressive capabilities of traditional Irish piping and solo or session instrumentation. Because its history and origins outside of Keenan's authorship are not widely documented, its legacy rests entirely on its adoption within modern traditional music repertoires.
