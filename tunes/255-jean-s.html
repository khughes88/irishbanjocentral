Jean's

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Jean's is a lively 4/4 reel in the key of G major composed by the prominent Scottish accordionist and bandleader Bobby MacLeod. Written in the traditional West Highland dance style, the tune features an upbeat tempo and bright phrasing well-suited for social dancing. Because specific historical associations for the piece are not well documented, it is primarily celebrated today through recordings of Scottish traditional dance and ceilidh music.
