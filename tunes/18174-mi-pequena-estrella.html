Mi Pequena Estrella

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Composed by acclaimed Irish fiddler Winifred Horan, Mi Pequena Estrella is a graceful contemporary waltz set in three-quarter time. The tune is written in the key of E minor, blending gentle melancholy with a flowing, lyrical melodic contour well suited for close listening or partner dancing. Because its history outside of Horan's authorship is sparsely documented, its traditional associations remain open while its modern fiddle-music pedigree is firmly established.
