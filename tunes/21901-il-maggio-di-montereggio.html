Il Maggio Di Montereggio

Polka · 2/4 · A minor

Il Maggio Di Montereggio is a traditional Italian polka from the Tuscany region, specifically associated with the village of Montereggio in the municipality of Mulazzo. The exact historical background and composer of the tune remain uncertain, but it is traditionally performed as a lively instrumental piece for accordion and other folk instruments during local May celebrations. Set in 2/4 meter and the key of A minor, it features a brisk, danceable rhythm and a festive character typical of rural Tuscan repertoire.
