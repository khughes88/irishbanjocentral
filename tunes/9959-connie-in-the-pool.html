Connie In The Pool

Polka · 2/4 · A major

Connie In The Pool is a 2/4 polka in the key of A major composed by the well-known Irish fiddler Séamus Creagh. As is characteristic of the polka genre in traditional Irish music, the tune features a lively, upbeat rhythm designed to drive social dancing. Because its exact historical associations are not widely documented, the tune is best appreciated for its buoyant melody and the distinctive regional style brought to it by its composer.
