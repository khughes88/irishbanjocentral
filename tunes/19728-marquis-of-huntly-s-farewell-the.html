Strathspey · 4/4 · A major

Composed by the renowned Scottish musician William Marshall, The Marquis Of Huntly's Farewell is a traditional strathspey in four-four meter and A major. The tune features the characteristic dotted rhythms and snapped notes typical of the strathspey genre, requiring a brisk, lifting bowing style.
