A Nation Once Again

March · 4/4 · D major

A Nation Once Again is an Irish patriotic march composed by Thomas Osborne Davis in the nineteenth century. Written in 4/4 meter and the key of D major, the tune features an uplifting and resolute character well-suited for its vocal and instrumental interpretations. It became one of the most popular and enduring nationalist anthems in Ireland, frequently performed at historical and political gatherings.
