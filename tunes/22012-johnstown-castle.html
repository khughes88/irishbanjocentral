Johnstown Castle

Reel · 4/4 · C major

Johnstown Castle is a traditional Irish reel composed by accordion player Paddy Hayes, who recorded it in the 1970s. Set in C major and in 4/4 meter, the tune follows the standard AABB reel structure common in traditional instrumental sessions. Its lively and upbeat character makes it a popular choice for contemporary performers exploring the repertoire of County Clare musicians.
