Soondachan MacPhee

Strathspey · 4/4 · A major

Soondachan MacPhee is a traditional-style strathspey composed by the accordionist and bandleader Bobby MacLeod. Written in A major and structured in a 4/4 meter, the tune features the characteristic dotted rhythms and driving lift required for traditional Scottish Highland dancing. Because precise historical associations beyond its creation by MacLeod are not widely documented, musicians focus on delivering its lively, rhythmic character with energetic phrasing.
