Gylen Castle

Strathspey · 4/4 · D major

Gylen Castle is a traditional Scottish strathspey in four-four meter and D major, though its exact composer and historical origins are not documented. The tune is characterized by the dotted rhythms and brisk, energetic style typical of traditional highland fiddle music. It is frequently performed today by Scottish traditional musicians celebrating the coastal fortress on the Isle of Kerrera after which it is named.
