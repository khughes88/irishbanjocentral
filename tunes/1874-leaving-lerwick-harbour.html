Leaving Lerwick Harbour

Reel · 4/4 · G minor

Leaving Lerwick Harbour is a contemporary 4/4 reel in G minor composed by the acclaimed Shetland fiddler Willie Hunter. The tune is celebrated for its driving rhythm and maritime-inspired character, reflecting the rich traditional fiddle style of the Shetland Islands. As a modern standard in the traditional repertoire, it is frequently performed by Celtic and Scottish musicians around the world.
