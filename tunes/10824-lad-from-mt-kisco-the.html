Lad From Mt. Kisco, The

Jig · 6/8 · D major

The Lad From Mt. Kisco is a traditional-style Irish double jig composed by the fiddle player Brendan Tonra while he was living in New York. Set in 6/8 meter and the key of D major, the tune features an upbeat and melodic character typical of modern compositions that seamlessly enter the traditional session repertoire. Tonra, a native of County Mayo, crafted the tune during his time in the United States, where it has since become widely popular among musicians performing Irish traditional music.
