Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Seventeenth of July is a contemporary waltz composed by Alan Griffin in the G major scale with a 3/4 meter. Because the tune is a modern composition rather than an older traditional piece, its historical background is defined by its creation within contemporary folk circles.
