Hornpipe · 4/4 · A major

Very Kind And Gracious is a contemporary hornpipe in 4/4 time and A major composed by Irish music specialist Grey Larsen. The tune is characterized by its lively, rhythmic drive and smooth lyrical phrasing typical of modern American and Irish-style traditional dance music.
