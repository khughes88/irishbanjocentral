Furry Flats

Jig · 6/8 · D major

Furry Flats is a contemporary jig in six-eight time and the key of D major composed by Shetland fiddle player Robbie Leask. The tune features the characteristic bouncy rhythm and lively character typical of traditional-style Shetland dance music. Written to showcase an uplifting melodic contour, it is commonly performed with a driving lift and crisp bowing style suited for dancing.
