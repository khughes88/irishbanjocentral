Cloch Na Ceithre Mhile

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Cloch Na Ceithre Mhile is a traditional Irish reel composed by the violinist and collector Arthur Darley. Written in 4/4 meter and set in A Dorian, the tune shares the spirited character and dance-driven rhythm typical of traditional fiddle music. Because specific historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, the tune is best known through its circulation in standard Irish traditional repertoire.
