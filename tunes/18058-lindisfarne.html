Lindisfarne

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Lindisfarne is a contemporary waltz in D major composed by Matt Seattle. Set in 3/4 meter, the tune is played with a graceful, flowing character typical of modern border-pipe and folk repertoire. While it does not carry an older historical association, it has become a well-regarded staple among modern folk musicians and dancers.
