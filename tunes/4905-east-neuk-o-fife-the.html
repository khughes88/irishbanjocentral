East Neuk O' Fife, The

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

The hornpipe East Neuk O' Fife is a traditional Scottish tune in 4/4 time and G major that was published by the Scottish composer James Oswald in the 18th century. Characterized by its lively and rhythmic character, it is well suited to traditional fiddle and bagpipe playing styles.
