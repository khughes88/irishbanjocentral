Burning House, The

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

The reel known as The Burning House was composed by the well-known Cape Breton fiddler and composer Brenda Stubbert. Written in 4/4 meter and set in A Dorian, the tune is characterized by a driving, rhythmic energy typical of modern Cape Breton fiddle music. Because specific historical associations beyond its composition are not established, the tune is primarily valued for its vibrant melody and its prominent place in contemporary fiddle repertoires.
