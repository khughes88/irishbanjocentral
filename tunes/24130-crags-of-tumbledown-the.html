Crags Of Tumbledown, The

March · 4/4 · A mixolydian

The 4/4 march The Crags Of Tumbledown was composed by James Riddell. The tune is written in A mixolydian and features a steady, driving rhythm characteristic of traditional bagpipe marches. It was composed during the Falklands War in 1982 by Pipe Major James Riddell of the Scots Guards, commemorating the regiment's assault on Mount Tumbledown.
