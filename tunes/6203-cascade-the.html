Cascade, The

Polka · 2/4 · G major

The Cascade is a traditional Irish polka composed by accordion player Timmy O'Connor. Set in 2/4 time and the key of G major, it features a lively and rhythmic character well-suited for traditional dance settings. Because specific historical associations beyond its composition are not widely documented, the tune is best defined by its bright melodic flow and standard session-playing style.
