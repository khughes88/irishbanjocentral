Humours Of Bandon, The

Jig · 6/8 · D major

The Humours of Bandon is a traditional Irish double jig set in six-eight time and the key of D major, with no verified composer of record. Characterized by its lively and rhythmic melody, it is commonly played for set dancing, particularly the traditional Set of Bandon. Because its early history and exact origins are uncertain, the tune is best defined by its enduring popularity within the traditional Irish repertoire.
