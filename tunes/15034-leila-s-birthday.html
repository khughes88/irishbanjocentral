Leila's Birthday

Reel · 4/4 · B minor

Leila's Birthday is a modern reel composed by contemporary fiddler Hanneke Cassel. Written in 4/4 meter and set in B minor, the tune features an upbeat character typical of Cassel's signature fusion of Scottish, Cape Breton, and American folk styles. It is frequently performed in contemporary fiddle circles and recorded within the modern Celtic and traditional music repertoires.
