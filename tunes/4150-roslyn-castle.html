Roslyn Castle

Reel · 4/4 · D minor

Roslyn Castle is a traditional Scottish tune that is widely found in eighteenth-century collections with an uncertain precise origin. It is characterized by a slow, lamenting melody in four-four time and D minor, often played with a solemn and expressive character. The tune gained significant historical popularity after being associated with the poignant lyrics written by Richard Hewitt in the mid-eighteenth century.
