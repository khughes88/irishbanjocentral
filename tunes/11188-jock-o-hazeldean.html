Jock O'Hazeldean

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Jock O'Hazeldean is a traditional Scottish tune associated with the Sir Walter Scott ballad of the same name. Written in 3/4 time as a waltz in the key of D major, it features a graceful and lyrical character well suited for dancing. Because the exact historical origins of the melody remain uncertain, it is most widely recognized through its nineteenth-century literary connections.
