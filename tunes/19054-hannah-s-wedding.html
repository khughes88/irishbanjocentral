Hannah's Wedding

March · 4/4 · B mixolydian

Hannah's Wedding is a contemporary march composed by modern Scottish and Cape Breton-style fiddler Hanneke Cassel. Set in 4/4 time and the key of B mixolydian, the tune features a driving, rhythmic cadence well-suited for lively step dancing and modern folk accompaniment. While it does not carry an older historical association, it has become a popular selection within the contemporary fiddle community since its publication.
