Garageschottis

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G mixolydian

Garageschottis is a contemporary hornpipe composed by Mikael Marin. Written in 4/4 meter and the G mixolydian mode, the tune reflects the lively and rhythmic character typical of modern Swedish folk dance music. Because specific historical associations and traditional backgrounds are not documented for this modern composition, it is appreciated purely for its engaging contemporary arrangement and fiddle style.
