Hökpers Vals

Waltz · 3/4 · D minor

Hökpers Vals is a traditional-style waltz composed by Lars Hökpers in the D minor key and set in 3/4 meter. The piece features the characteristic flowing rhythm and expressive melodic contour typical of Swedish folk waltzes. As the specific historical associations and exact date of composition are not widely documented, the tune is best known through its continued performance in contemporary traditional fiddle repertoires.
