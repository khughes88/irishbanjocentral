Heart Of The Heartland

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Heart Of The Heartland is a contemporary waltz composed by the late American mandolinist and fiddler Peter Ostroushko. Written in 3/4 meter and set in the key of G major, the piece carries a gentle, lyrical character that reflects the melodic warmth and folk-infused sensibilities of Upper Midwest acoustic music. The tune is widely performed by traditional and contemporary folk musicians as both a lyrical listening piece and a graceful dance melody.
