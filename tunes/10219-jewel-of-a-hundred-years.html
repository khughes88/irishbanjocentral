Jewel Of A Hundred Years

March · 4/4 · D major

Jewel Of A Hundred Years is a contemporary 4/4 march in D major composed by the Scottish accordionist Phil Cunningham. The tune is characterized by its bright, uplifting melody and steady rhythmic drive suited for march tempos. It has become a popular addition to modern Celtic repertoire among both session players and contemporary folk ensembles.
