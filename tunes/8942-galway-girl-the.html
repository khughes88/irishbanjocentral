Galway Girl, The

Reel · 4/4 · D major

The Galway Girl is a contemporary Irish-style reel composed by American singer-songwriter Steve Earle during his time living in the west of Ireland. Written in 4/4 meter and D major, the tune features a lively, driving rhythm characteristic of modern Irish traditional sessions. It gained widespread popularity after being featured on Earle's 2000 album Transcendental Blues and later popularized further by the band Beoga.
