Battle Of Killiecrankie, The

March · 4/4 · G major

The Battle of Killiecrankie is a traditional Scottish tune associated with the historic 1689 battle fought during the first Jacobite rising. Set in 4/4 meter and G major, it is played as a lively march with a driving, energetic rhythm suited to traditional piping and fiddling. Because its exact authorship is not recorded, the tune remains anonymous while enduring as a prominent piece in the Scottish martial repertoire.
