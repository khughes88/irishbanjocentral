Stone Outside Dan Murphy's Door, The

Waltz · 3/4 · A major

The waltz Stone Outside Dan Murphy's Door was composed by the 19th-century Irish singer-songwriter Johnny Patterson. Written in 3/4 time and set in the key of A major, the tune reflects the lyrical and narrative style characteristic of traditional Irish parlour music and music hall songs. While its exact early performance history remains largely undocumented outside of Patterson's theatrical career, the melody has endured as a popular choice in Irish traditional music sessions.
