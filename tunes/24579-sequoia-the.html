Sequoia, The

Slip jig · 9/8 · E dorian

Composed by Yoann Filin, The Sequoia is a contemporary slip jig set in 9/8 time and the key of E dorian. The tune is characterized by a lively and rhythmic folk dance feel that suits the traditional Irish and Celtic instrumental style. Because it is a modern composition, it does not carry an older historical association and remains a notable addition to the contemporary repertoire.
