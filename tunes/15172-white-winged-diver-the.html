White Winged Diver, The

Jig · 6/8 · G major

The White Winged Diver is a contemporary 6/8 jig composed by accordionist Aaron Collis of the Newfoundland traditional music group Rum Ragged. Written in G major, the tune blends the lively rhythmic bounce characteristic of traditional maritime dance music with a modern melodic sensibility. While it does not carry an older historical association, it has become a notable addition to the contemporary Newfoundland folk and trad repertoire through recordings and live performances.
