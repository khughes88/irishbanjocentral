Helston Furry Dance, The

Reel · 4/4 · G major

The Helston Furry Dance is a well-established traditional English processional tune associated with the annual Flora Day festival held in Helston, Cornwall. Written in 4/4 meter and G major, it functions as a lively reel with a straightforward, celebratory character designed for group dancing through the town's streets. While its exact historical origins remain uncertain, the melody has served as the central accompaniment for this historic May 8th community celebration since at least the late eighteenth century.
