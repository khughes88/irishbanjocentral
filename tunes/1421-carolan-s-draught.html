Carolan's Draught

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Carolan's Draught is a well-known traditional Irish tune composed by the blind harper Turlough O'Carolan in the early eighteenth century. Written in the key of G major with a 4/4 meter, it is commonly played as a lively and spirited reel. While specific historical events linked to the tune's creation remain uncertain, it stands as a celebrated example of O'Carolan's unique blending of traditional Gaelic harping styles with Baroque musical influences.
