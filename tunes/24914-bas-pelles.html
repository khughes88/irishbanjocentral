Bas Pelles

Three-two · 3/2 · F dorian

Bas Pelle is a Swedish three-two tune composed by the fiddler Per Westberg. Written in the F dorian mode with a 3/2 meter, it features the characteristic groove and phrasing typical of traditional Swedish folk dance music. Because specific historical associations and the exact date of composition are not documented, the piece is best appreciated through its distinctive modal harmony and rhythmic drive.
