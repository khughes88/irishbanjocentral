Tee Tie Tum Tittle Tee

Jig · 6/8 · D mixolydian

Tee Tie Tum Tittle Tee is a modern jig composed by Natalie MacMaster and Matt MacIsaac. Written in 6/8 meter and D mixolydian mode, the tune exhibits the lively rhythm and energetic character typical of contemporary Cape Breton fiddle music. Because it is a recently composed piece, it lacks the deep historical associations of older traditional tunes, but it remains a vibrant addition to modern Celtic repertoires.
