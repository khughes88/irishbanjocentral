Zonaradikos

Jig · 6/8 · D major

Zonaradikos is a traditional Greek circle dance and tune from the Thrace region with an uncertain exact origin and no known single composer. It is typically played in a lively 6/8 meter in D major, featuring a driving rhythm that supports energetic communal dancing. Because its historical emergence predates modern recording, specific dates and historical associations remain largely undocumented in scholarly sources.
