Fiddler's Green

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Fiddler's Green is a modern folk waltz composed by English singer-songwriter John Conolly in 1970, capturing the mythical sailor's paradise where the beer flows free and the work is never done. Written in G major with a 3/4 meter, the tune features a gentle, flowing rhythm that complements its reflective and lyrical maritime character. While conceived as a contemporary song, it has since been widely adopted by traditional musicians and instrumentalists as a beloved session waltz.
