St. James' Kitchen

Reel · 4/4 · G major

St. James Kitchen is a modern G major reel composed by the well-known flute and whistle player Brendan Tonra, who was born in County Mayo and later settled in Boston. As a traditional-style Irish reel in 4/4 meter, it is structured for lively dance accompaniment with a bright, energetic character and a steady, driving rhythm.
