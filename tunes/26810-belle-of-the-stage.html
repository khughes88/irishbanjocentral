Hornpipe · 4/4 · D major

Composed by Edwin Christie, Belle Of The Stage is a traditional hornpipe set in the key of D major with a standard 4/4 meter. The tune features the characteristic dotted rhythms and lively, upbeat tempo typical of nineteenth-century stage hornpipes.
