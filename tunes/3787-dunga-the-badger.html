Dunga & The Badger

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Dunga and the Badger is a contemporary reel in four-four time composed by Brian Morrissey. Set in the key of E minor, the tune is driven by a lively and rhythmic character suited for traditional Irish session playing. Because detailed historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, its reception relies entirely on its modern popularity among performers of Irish traditional music.
