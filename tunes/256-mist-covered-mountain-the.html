Mist Covered Mountain, The

Jig · 6/8 · A dorian

The Mist Covered Mountain is a well-known Irish jig composed by the fiddle and flute player Junior Crehan from County Clare. Written in 6/8 meter and set in A Dorian, the tune has a flowing, lyrical character that evokes the misty landscapes of its origin. It has since become a staple of the traditional Irish session repertoire and is widely recorded by contemporary folk musicians.
