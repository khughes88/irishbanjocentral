Mrs Grace Bowie

Jig · 6/8 · A major

Mrs Grace Bowie is a traditional-style 6/8 jig composed by the accordionist and dance band leader Adam Rennie. Written in the key of A major, the tune features the lively rhythm and bright, uplifting character typical of Scottish country dance music. Because detailed historical associations for the piece are not widely documented, it is primarily valued today as a spirited addition to the accordion and fiddle repertoires.
