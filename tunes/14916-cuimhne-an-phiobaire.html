Cuimhne An Phíobaire

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Cuimhne An Phíobaire, translating as The Piper's Memory, is a waltz composed by the renowned Irish musician Sean Potts. Written in three-four meter and set in the key of E minor, the tune features a graceful and expressive melodic character suited to slow, flowing interpretation. Because specific historical associations beyond its creation by Potts are not well established, musicians focus on its lyrical phrasing and poignant mood during performance.
