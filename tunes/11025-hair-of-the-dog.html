Hair Of The Dog

Reel · 4/4 · A mixolydian

Hair Of The Dog is a modern reel composed by Alan MacLeod. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of A mixolydian, the tune features an energetic and driving character suited for traditional Scottish sessions. Because it is a contemporary composition, it carries no older historical associations, but it has become a popular addition to modern fiddling and piping repertoires.
