Caoineadh Cu Chulainn

Polka · 2/4 · G major

Caoineadh Cu Chulainn was composed by the Irish uilleann piper and low whistle player Davy Spillane for the 1995 film soundtrack of Quigley Down Under. Although classified in data as a polka in 2/4 meter with a G major key signature, the piece is renowned as a deeply mournful, expressive lament rather than a traditional dance tune. Because of its modern cinematic origin, it lacks the historical associations of older folklore, yet it has since become a widely embraced standard in contemporary traditional Irish music.
