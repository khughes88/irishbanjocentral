New Concertina, The

Reel · 4/4 · A major

The New Concertina is a traditional Irish reel in 4/4 time and the key of A major, though its specific geographic origin and composer remain unknown. The tune is characterized by an upbeat, danceable rhythm and a lively, nimble melodic contour well-suited to traditional button accordion and concertina playing. It is frequently featured in modern traditional music sessions and recordings by various Irish ensemble artists.
