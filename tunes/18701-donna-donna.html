Donna Donna

Barndance · 4/4 · E minor

Donna Donna is a well-known Yiddish song composed by Sholom Secunda with lyrics by Aaron Zeitlin, originally written for a 1940 Yiddish theater production. Although often performed with a gentle, flowing character in various tempos, it has been adapted across numerous genres and instrumental styles worldwide. Despite being structured in 4/4 meter and performed as a barndance in traditional instrumental repertoires, its historical roots remain firmly tied to the theatrical stage rather than folk dance origins.
