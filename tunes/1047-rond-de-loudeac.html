Rond De Loudéac

Reel · 4/4 · B minor

Rond de Loudéac is a traditional circle dance tune originating from the Loudéac region of Brittany in France. Set in four-four meter and usually played in B minor, the melody features a steady, driving rhythm suited for traditional Breton chain and circle dances. Because its exact historical origins and authorship are not documented, musicians focus on its lively character and rhythmic modal drive during festive performances known as festoù-noz.
