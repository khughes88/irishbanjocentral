A Scone For Breakfast

Reel · 4/4 · G major

A Scone For Breakfast is a contemporary reel composed in G major by Robert Winokur. Written in 4/4 meter, the tune features the lively and driving rhythmic character typical of standard session reels. Because it is a modern composition by a known fiddler, it lacks the obscure origins of older traditional tunes and is frequently played in Irish and Scottish sessions.
