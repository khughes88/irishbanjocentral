Hanter Dro

Slide · 12/8 · B minor

Hanter Dro is a modern composition by Tiennet Simonnin and Ronan Le Dissez that adapts a traditional Breton dance form. Written in B minor with a 12/8 meter and classified as a slide, it features the driving, cyclical character typical of modern Breton tune styling. Because it is a contemporary creation rather than an anonymous folk melody, it does not carry older historical associations.
