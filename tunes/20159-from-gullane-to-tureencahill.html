From Gullane To Tureencahill

Polka · 2/4 · D major

From Gullane To Tureencahill is a traditional Irish polka composed by accordion player Bryan O'Leary, bridging musical styles from East Kerry and Sliabh Luachra. Set in 2/4 meter and D major, the tune features the lively, driving rhythm and dotted phrasing characteristic of Sliabh Luachra dance music. It is frequently performed in contemporary traditional sessions by musicians inspired by the vibrant repertoire of the Cork and Kerry border region.
