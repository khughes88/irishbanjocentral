En Revenant De Sept​-​Îles

Reel · 4/4 · G major

En Revenant De Sept-Îles is a traditional French-Canadian reel in 4/4 time and G major. Played with the energetic bounce characteristic of traditional fiddling, it is designed to accompany lively social dancing. Because its exact historical origins and composer are not documented, it survives purely through oral tradition as a standard part of the fiddle repertoire.
