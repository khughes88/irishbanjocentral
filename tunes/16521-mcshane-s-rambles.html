McShane's Rambles

Jig · 6/8 · D dorian

McShane's Rambles is a traditional jig in 6/8 meter that was composed by the Donegal fiddler and singer Packie Manus Byrne. Set in the key of D dorian, the tune features an engaging rhythm and an expressive character suited to traditional Irish ensemble and solo performance.
