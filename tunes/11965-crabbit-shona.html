Crabbit Shona

Jig · 6/8 · D major

Crabbit Shona is a contemporary 6/8 jig in the key of D major composed by the Scottish fiddle player Angus R. Grant. Because its history and associations are not widely documented in historical records, the tune is best known through its energetic modern performances in traditional Scottish sessions. It is characterized by a lively, spirited rhythm and a driving playing style typical of contemporary highland fiddle music.
