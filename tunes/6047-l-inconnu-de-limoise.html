L'Inconnu De Limoise

Mazurka · 3/4 · C major

L'Inconnu De Limoise is a modern French mazurka composed by Jean François Heintzen in the key of C major. Written in 3/4 time, the tune features the graceful, flowing rhythm and lyrical character typical of contemporary European folk mazurkas. Because it is a recently composed piece rather than an older traditional melody, it lacks deep historical associations and is primarily played in modern folk dance and bal folk settings.
