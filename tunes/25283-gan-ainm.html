Gan Ainm

Hornpipe · 4/4 · C major

Gan Ainm is an Irish hornpipe played in four-four time and set in the key of C major, with its specific origin and composer remaining unknown. The title itself is the Irish Gaelic term for "without name," which is commonly used in traditional Irish music collections for anonymous tunes. Because of its lively rhythm and bright character, musicians frequently include it in sessions as a spirited instrumental dance piece.
