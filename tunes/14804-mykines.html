Mykines

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Mykines is a contemporary waltz composed by the Irish musician and composer Máire Breatnach. Written in three-four meter and set in the key of E minor, the piece features a flowing, melodic character typical of modern Celtic fiddle compositions. Because it is a recent instrumental work rather than an older folk tune, it does not carry traditional historical associations or anonymous roots.
