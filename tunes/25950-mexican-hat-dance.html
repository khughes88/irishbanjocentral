Mexican Hat Dance

Jig · 6/8 · A major

The Mexican Hat Dance is a traditional folk tune and the national dance of Mexico, with origins rooted in various regional zapateado styles of the nineteenth century. The piece features a lively 6/8 meter in the key of A major, characterized by an upbeat, playful rhythm that traditionally accompanies foot-stomping choreography. Although its exact composer is unknown, the tune gained widespread international recognition and official status as Mexico's national folk dance during the early twentieth century.
