Johnny I Hardly Knew Ya

Jig · 6/8 · E minor

Johnny I Hardly Knew Ya is a traditional Irish anti-war song and tune of uncertain early origin, though it gained widespread popularity in the nineteenth century. Set in 6/8 meter as a lively jig in E minor, it pairs an upbeat, driving tempo with dark and mournful lyrical themes. The tune is best known musically for its infectious rhythmic bounce, which contrasts sharply with its famous tragic narrative of a soldier returning home maimed from war.
