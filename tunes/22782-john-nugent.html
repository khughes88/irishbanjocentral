John Nugent

Jig · 6/8 · D minor

John Nugent is an Irish jig composed in the key of D minor by the blind harper Turlough O'Carolan during the early eighteenth century. Written in 6/8 meter, the tune features the characteristic lyrical and flowing melodic style typical of O'Carolan's extensive catalog of compositions. While the precise historical circumstances surrounding its dedication remain uncertain, the piece has endured as a popular and celebrated item in the traditional Irish repertoire.
