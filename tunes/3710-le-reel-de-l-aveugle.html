Le Reel De L'aveugle

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Le Reel De L'aveugle is a traditional French-Canadian reel played in 4/4 time with a lively and upbeat character. Because the composer and exact historical origins are not documented, the tune is best known through traditional performance practice in the fiddle repertoire. Musically, it is set in D major and features the brisk tempo, driving rhythm, and ornate bowing characteristic of traditional Quebecois fiddle music.
