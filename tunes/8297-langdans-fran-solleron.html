Långdans Från Sollerön

Waltz · 3/4 · D minor

Langdans fran Solleron is a traditional Swedish folk tune from the parish of Solleron in Dalarna. Played in 3/4 meter as a waltz, the piece features a characteristic minor-key melody in D minor with a steady, rhythmic pulse suited for traditional dancing. Because its exact composer and precise historical origins are not documented, the tune is preserved primarily through oral tradition and local fiddler repertoires.
