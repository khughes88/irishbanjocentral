4 Minutes 33 Seconds

Mazurka · 3/4 · E dorian

Written by John Cage, 4 Minutes 33 Seconds is an avant-garde musical work that departs entirely from traditional forms like the mazurka, existing instead as a silent piece composed of ambient sounds heard during performance. This unconventional composition unfolds in 3/4 meter within an E dorian framework only conceptually, as the score instructs performers not to play their instruments for the duration. First performed in 1952 by pianist David Tudor, the piece famously challenges traditional definitions of music by framing silence and environmental noise as the primary sonic content.
