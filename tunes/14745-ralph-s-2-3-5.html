Reel · 4/4 · E minor

The tune is characterized by its driving modern rhythm, intricate phrasing, and lively, expressive character that demands technical agility from performers. Although specific historical associations beyond its creation by Carroll are not documented, it has become a popular piece within contemporary traditional music sessions.
