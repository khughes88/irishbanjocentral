Braemar Gathering March, The

Jig · 6/8 · A major

The Braemar Gathering March is a 6/8 jig composed in A major by the renowned Scottish piper and composer G.S. MacLennan. It is characterized by the lively, bouncing rhythm and driving character typical of traditional Scottish bagpipe marches adapted for dancing or processional use. While specific historical documentation linking the tune to a particular event beyond its namesake gathering is limited, it remains a celebrated piece within the competitive bagpipe repertoire.
