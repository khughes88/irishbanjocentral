Tibetan National Anthem

Reel · 4/4 · G major

The Tibetan National Anthem, known as Gangri Rawa, is a modern composition whose lyrics were written by the fourteenth Dalai Lama in the mid-twentieth century with a musical setting adopted later. While designated as a reel in 4/4 meter and G major, this patriotic song functions as a formal anthem rather than a standard dance tune. Because its specific historical and compositional background remains complex and largely undocumented in traditional dance repertoires, it is best appreciated through its solemn character and cultural significance.
