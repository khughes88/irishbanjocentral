Imperial Quick Step, The

March · 4/4 · D major

The Imperial Quick Step is a traditional march tune in 4/4 meter and the key of D major. Because its specific composer and historical origins are not documented, it is best known through its circulation in nineteenth-century tunebooks and manuscript collections. The tune is characterized by a bright, steady, and uplifting tempo typical of military-style quicksteps designed for marching accompaniment.
