Glencoe, The

March · 4/4 · D major

Composed by the renowned Scottish-Canadian fiddler Dan R. MacDonald, The Glencoe is a traditional-style 4/4 march set in the key of D major. The tune is characterized by a strong, steady rhythmic drive typical of Scottish Highland and Cape Breton march playing, featuring crisp dotted rhythms and a stirring, martial character. Because the tune's specific historical associations beyond its creation by MacDonald are not widely documented, performers focus on its traditional structural architecture and lively phrasing to deliver an authentic rendition.
