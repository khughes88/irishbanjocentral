Marche Du Cheval Blanc

Mazurka · 3/4 · E dorian

Marche Du Cheval Blanc is a traditional-style mazurka composed by Christian Faucheur. Written in 3/4 meter and set in E dorian, the piece features the distinctive triple-meter rhythm and lively, danceable character typical of the genre. Because detailed historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, the tune is best known through its performance style within the modern folk repertoire.
