Mná Na hÉireann

Barndance · 4/4 · G major

Mná Na hÉireann is a well-known traditional Irish tune composed by Seán Ó Riada. Although structurally classified as a barndance in 4/4 meter and G major, it is most famously performed as a slow, expressive air. Because of its haunting and evocative character, the tune has been recorded by numerous artists and featured in several historical film soundtracks.
