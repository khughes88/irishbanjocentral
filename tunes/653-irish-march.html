Irish March

Jig · 6/8 · G major

Irish March is a six-eight jig in G major composed by the English Renaissance keyboard master William Byrd. The piece is characterized by its lively compound duple meter and the ornate, idiomatic keyboard style typical of the period's virginal music. While its exact date of composition remains uncertain, it is preserved in significant historical sources such as the Fitzwilliam Virginal Book.
