Isles Of Gletness, The

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

The waltz known as The Isles Of Gletness was composed by John Barclay. Set in three-four meter and G major, it exhibits the graceful rhythm and lyrical character typical of traditional Shetland fiddle music. Because specific historical associations for the piece are not well documented, its significance rests entirely on its enduring melodic charm within the regional repertoire.
