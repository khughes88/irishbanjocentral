Red Socks

March · 4/4 · G major

Red Socks is a 4/4 march in G major composed by the well-known Scottish accordionist and bandleader Fergie MacDonald. As a modern accordion march, the tune features a bright, driving rhythm and an energetic character tailored for Scottish country dancing and ceilidh performances. Because detailed historical associations for the piece are not widely documented, its enduring popularity relies primarily on its lively melodic appeal within contemporary traditional music circles.
