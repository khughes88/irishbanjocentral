Angels Candles

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Angels Candles is a contemporary waltz composed by the renowned Irish fiddler and violist Máire Breatnach. Written in three-four meter and set in the key of E minor, the tune features a gentle, flowing melodic character typical of modern Celtic slow waltzes. Because the tune is a recent composition rather than an older folk piece, it does not carry historical associations from traditional lore and is generally performed in contemporary acoustic and folk settings.
