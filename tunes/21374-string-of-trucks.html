String Of Trucks

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

String Of Trucks is a contemporary reel in four-four time composed by Jim Kimball. Written in the E dorian mode, the tune features an energetic and driving character well-suited for traditional fiddle and session playing. Detailed historical associations and the exact date of composition are not widely documented, leaving the focus on its lively rhythmic style.
