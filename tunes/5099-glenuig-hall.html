Glenuig Hall

Slip jig · 9/8 · G major

Glenuig Hall is a contemporary slip jig in 9/8 meter and G major composed by Luke Plumb. As a modern tune written for a well-known community hall in Moidart, Scotland, it lacks a traditional historical background or older associations. The piece features the characteristic lively, flowing triple-time rhythm of a slip jig, designed for an agile and uplifting playing style.
