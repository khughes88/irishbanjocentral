Cape Breton Fiddlers' Society's Welcome To The Shetland Islands, The

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Composed by the renowned Shetland fiddler Willie Hunter, The Cape Breton Fiddlers' Society's Welcome To The Shetland Islands is a lively 4/4 reel in the key of G major. The tune is characterized by a bright, driving rhythm and an uplifting melodic style typical of contemporary Shetland fiddle repertoire. It reflects the strong cultural and musical connections shared between traditional fiddlers in Shetland and Cape Breton.
