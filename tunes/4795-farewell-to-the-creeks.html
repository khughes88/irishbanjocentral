Farewell To The Creeks

Jig · 6/8 · A mixolydian

Farewell To The Creeks is a traditional Scottish pipe march composed by James Robertson, though it is frequently played with the lively compound meter and energetic drive characteristic of a six-eight jig. The tune is typically performed in A Mixolydian mode, featuring the bright, modal tonality common to Highland bagpipe repertoire. While its precise historical associations beyond twentieth-century piping collections remain largely undocumented, it has become a popular staple in competitive solo piping and pipe band repertoires worldwide.
