Paddy Taylor's

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Paddy Taylor's is a traditional Irish reel composed by the esteemed flute player and fiddler Paddy Taylor, who was born in County Limerick and later settled in County Clare. Written in 4/4 meter and the key of G major, the tune features a lively, fluid character well-suited to the ornamented, expressive style characteristic of West Clare traditional music. Although exact dating for its composition remains uncertain, it has become a widely established and popular staple in modern Irish traditional session repertoires.
