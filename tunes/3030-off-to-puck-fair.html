Jig · 6/8 · D major

Off to Puck Fair is a traditional Irish double jig set in six-eight meter and the key of D major. Because the composer and exact historical origins are not recorded, the tune is best known through its association with Ireland's oldest traditional gathering, the Puck Fair held annually in County Kerry. It is typically played with a lively and rhythmic character that suits traditional step dancing and lively pub sessions alike.
