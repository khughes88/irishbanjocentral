Laird O' Cockpen, The

Jig · 6/8 · D major

The Laird O' Cockpen is a traditional Scottish jig in six-eight meter and D major. Because its exact origin and composer are not historically established, it is best known through its widespread performance in traditional fiddle and dance repertoires. Characterized by its lively lilt and upbeat rhythm, the tune is typically played in a bright, driving style well-suited for social dancing.
