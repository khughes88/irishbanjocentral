Cap And Bells

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Cap And Bells is a traditional-style reel in 4/4 time and D major composed by the well-known Irish fiddler and pianist Josephine Keegan. Because detailed historical records regarding the exact circumstances of its composition are scarce, the tune is best defined by its lively melodic character and suitability for energetic dancing.
