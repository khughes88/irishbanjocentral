Each Little Thing

Jig · 6/8 · E major

Each Little Thing is a contemporary 6/8 jig composed by the prominent Irish-based musician Steve Cooney. Set in the key of E major, the tune exemplifies Cooney's innovative approach to traditional-style composition with its lively and rhythmic character. Because the tune is of recent origin, it lacks the older historical associations typical of traditional folk repertoire.
