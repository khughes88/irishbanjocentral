My Wild Irish Rose

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

My Wild Irish Rose is a popular American waltz written in 1899 by the songwriter and performer Chauncey Olcott for his Broadway production A Romance of Athlone. Composed in 3/4 meter and typically played in the key of D major, the tune features a sentimental, flowing melodic character suited for lyrical interpretation. It quickly became a standard in the Irish-American vocal and traditional music repertoires, remaining widely performed and recorded across generations.
