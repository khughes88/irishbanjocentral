Le 24 Juin

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Le 24 Juin is a traditional reel composed by renowned French-Canadian accordionist Philippe Bruneau in 4/4 meter and G major. Written to celebrate the Fête nationale du Québec, the tune is characterized by its lively tempo, bouncy rhythmic drive, and distinctively accordion-friendly phrasing. It has since become a standard staple in the traditional Québécois fiddle and folk repertoire, widely performed at sessions and community dances.
