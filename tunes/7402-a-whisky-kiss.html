A Whisky Kiss

Reel · 4/4 · A mixolydian

A Whisky Kiss is a modern Scottish reel composed by Iain M. MacLeod in 4/4 meter. The tune is written in the A mixolydian mode, giving it a bright and energetic character well-suited for lively contra and ceilidh dancing. Because it is a contemporary composition rather than an old folk tune, it does not carry traditional historical associations.
