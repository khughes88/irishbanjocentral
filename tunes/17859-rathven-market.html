Rathven Market

Hornpipe · 4/4 · D mixolydian

Rathven Market is a hornpipe composed by Ian Duncan. It is written in 4/4 time and set in the D mixolydian mode. Because specific historical associations for the tune are not widely documented, its character is best appreciated through its lively rhythmic drive typical of traditional Scottish dance music.
