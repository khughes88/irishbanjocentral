Molly's Balloon

March · 4/4 · D major

Molly's Balloon is a contemporary 4/4 march in D major composed by Aileen Kennedy. Because detailed historical associations and the exact circumstances of its origin are not widely documented, the tune is best defined by its lively musical character. It is played with the crisp articulation and steady, driving pulse typical of traditional-style bagpipe marches.
