Day Of The Sun's Rebirth

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

Day Of The Sun's Rebirth is a modern reel in 4/4 time composed by Cathal Croke, Conor Broderick, and Kevin Meehan. Written in the E dorian mode, the tune features an energetic and driving rhythmic character typical of contemporary Irish traditional music. Because it is a recently composed piece, it does not carry older historical associations and is primarily performed in modern sessions.
