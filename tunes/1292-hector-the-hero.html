Hector The Hero

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Composed by the renowned Scottish fiddler James Scott Skinner, Hector the Hero is a poignant waltz written in D major and set in three-four meter. Skinner penned the tune as a lament dedicated to Major-General Hector MacDonald following his tragic death in 1903. Characterized by a slow, expressive, and deeply emotional melody, it remains a beloved staple of traditional Scottish repertoire, frequently performed at solemn gatherings and Highland games.
