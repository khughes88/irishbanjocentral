Spotted Pony, The

Barndance · 4/4 · D major

The Spotted Pony is a traditional American fiddle tune played in 4/4 meter with the spirited, driving rhythm characteristic of old-time barndances. Because its exact historical origins and composer are not definitively documented, the tune is best known through its widespread preservation in regional Appalachian and Ozark oral traditions.
