Ae Fond Kiss

Waltz · 3/4 · A major

Ae Fond Kiss is a well-known Scottish song with lyrics written by Robert Burns in 1791 to the traditional melody known as Rory Dall's Port. The tune is typically performed as a gentle waltz in 3/4 time within the key of A major, reflecting a characteristically lyrical and expressive mood. Its enduring historical association stems from Burns's poignant farewell to his parting lover, Nancy McLehose, which immortalized the melody in Scottish culture.
