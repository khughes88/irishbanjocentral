Tarantella

Jig · 6/8 · A minor

Tarantella is a traditional Italian folk tune and dance set in six-eight meter and the key of A minor. Its musical character is fast, lively, and energetic, historically associated with a frenzied folk dance once believed to cure the venomous bite of a tarantula spider. Because the tune's exact historical origins remain uncertain, it is widely performed in various regional styles across southern Italy rather than tied to a single composer.
