Memories Of East Clare

Reel · 4/4 · D dorian

Memories Of East Clare is a traditional-style reel composed by accordion player Damien Connolly. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the D dorian mode, the tune features an energetic and driving rhythmic character well-suited for traditional Irish session playing. Because specific historical associations and older archival records for this modern composition are not established, the tune is best known through contemporary performances in the tradition.
