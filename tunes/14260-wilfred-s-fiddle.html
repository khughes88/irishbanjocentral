Jig · 6/8 · E dorian

Wilf's Fiddle is a 6/8 jig in E dorian composed by the renowned Cape Breton fiddler and tune-maker Dan R. MacDonald. The tune is characterized by its lively traditional Scottish-style phrasing and rhythmic drive, which are typical of MacDonald's extensive contribution to maritime fiddle music.
