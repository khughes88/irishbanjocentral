Keel Row, The

Polka · 2/4 · G major

The traditional Scottish and Northumbrian tune The Keel Row was published with a 19th-century arrangement by Joseph Lowe in his collection of native airs. Set in G major with a lively 2/4 meter, this upbeat polka is traditionally performed with a brisk tempo and spirited character suited for dancing. Because its exact historical origins before the 19th century remain uncertain, the tune is best known through its enduring popularity in regional fiddle and pipe repertoires.
