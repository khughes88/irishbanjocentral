Time Will End

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Time Will End is a contemporary reel in 4/4 time and G major composed by accordionist and pianist Jeremiah McLane. The tune features an uplifting and driving rhythmic character well-suited for lively contra dance and traditional music settings. Because the piece was newly composed for the modern folk idiom, it does not carry older historical associations.
