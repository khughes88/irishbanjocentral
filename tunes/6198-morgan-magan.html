Morgan Magan

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Morgan Magan is a well-known traditional Irish tune composed by the blind harper Turlough O'Carolan in the 18th century. Set in G major and 4/4 meter, the piece is structurally classified as a reel, though it reflects O'Carolan's distinctive blend of traditional Irish modal elements and Baroque compositional influences. The melody is celebrated for its graceful, flowing character and lyrical sweep, making it a favorite among both traditional instrumentalists and contemporary performers of early Irish music.
