Come Out, Ye Black And Tans

Polka · 2/4 · D dorian

Come Out, Ye Black And Tans is a well-known Irish rebel song written by Dominic Behan in the mid-twentieth century to mock the British auxiliary police force deployed to Ireland in the 1920s. Although originally structured as a vocal ballad rather than a traditional instrumental polka, it is sometimes adapted into a lively 2/4 rhythm for sessions. Its lyrics and catchy melody reflect the enduring legacy of Irish republican sentiment and anti-colonial resistance.
