Star Of The County Down

Jig · 6/8 · E minor

Star of the County Down is a traditional Irish song set to a lively 6/8 meter in the key of E minor, typically performed in the style of a spirited single jig. The tune features a memorable, rolling melodic contour that naturally accommodates narrative verse structures as well as instrumental dance settings.
