Changeling, The

Reel · 4/4 · A mixolydian

The Changeling is a traditional Irish reel in four-four meter and A Mixolydian mode, though its exact geographic origin and specific historical background remain uncertain. The tune is characterized by its lively tempo and playful yet driving rhythmic cadence well-suited for traditional step dancing. Because no historical composer attribution is verified, it is preserved strictly as part of the traditional folk repertoire and commonly played with a bright, articulated fiddle style.
