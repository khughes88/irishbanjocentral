Jig · 6/8 · D major

Newtown Bridge is a lively six-eight jig composed by the renowned mid-century button accordion player Paddy O'Brien. Written in the key of D major, the tune is characterized by its bright, uplifting melody and a bouncy traditional rhythm well suited for dancing.
