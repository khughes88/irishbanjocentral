Metsäkukkia

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Metsäkukkia is a well-known Finnish waltz whose exact composer and early history remain uncertain, as it is traditionally treated as an anonymous folk tune. Composed in 3/4 meter and set in E minor, the piece features a lyrical, melancholic melody with the graceful flow characteristic of Nordic folk waltzes. Over the decades, it has become a staple of the Finnish pelimanni fiddle tradition and has been recorded by numerous accordionists and folk ensembles.
