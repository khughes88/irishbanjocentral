Fishnet Jig, The

Slip jig · 9/8 · D mixolydian

The Fishnet Jig is a contemporary slip jig in 9/8 meter composed by Irish musician Mick McAuley. Set in the key of D mixolydian, the tune features the characteristic lurching and fluid rhythm typical of slip jigs. It is frequently performed in traditional Irish music settings with a lively and dynamic playing style.
