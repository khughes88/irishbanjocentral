Breton

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Breton is a contemporary waltz composed by Erwan Hamon within the modern Breton traditional music scene. Written in 3/4 meter and the key of E minor, the tune features a flowing, lyrical character typical of contemporary European folk waltzes. While it does not carry an older historical association, it is frequently performed in acoustic folk settings and fest-noz dance repertoires.
