Farewell To Ireland

Waltz · 3/4 · A dorian

Farewell to Ireland is a traditional Irish tune, though its exact origin, composer, and early history remain uncertain due to a lack of documented records. Despite its common association with a waltz meter in 3/4 time, the melody is more frequently encountered in Irish traditional music sessions as a reel in 4/4 time. In its waltz setting, the tune features the distinctive modal coloring of the A dorian key, lending it a poignant and expressive character well suited for solo or ensemble performance.
