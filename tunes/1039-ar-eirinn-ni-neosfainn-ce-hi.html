Ar Éirinn Ní Neosfainn Cé Hí

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Ar Éirinn Ní Neosfainn Cé Hí is a traditional Irish air whose specific origin and composer are not historically documented. Played as a waltz in 3/4 meter and G major, it features a deeply expressive and lyrical character suited to slow, contemplative performance. The tune translates from Irish as For Ireland I Would Not Tell Who She Is, reflecting the genre of aisling poetry where Ireland is personified as a woman.
