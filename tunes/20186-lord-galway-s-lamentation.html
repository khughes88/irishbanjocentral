Lord Galway's Lamentation

Waltz · 3/4 · D dorian

Lord Galway's Lamentation is a traditional Irish waltz composed in the early eighteenth century by the blind harpist Turlough O'Carolan. Written in 3/4 meter and the D dorian mode, the tune possesses a graceful yet mournful character that reflects the expressive style of late-period Gaelic harp music. Because specific details regarding its dedication and early performance history remain largely unverified, scholars primarily appreciate the piece for its refined melodic craftsmanship.
