Cor Natailí

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Cor Natailí is a traditional Irish reel in 4/4 time and G major. Because the composer and precise historical origins are not recorded, the tune is best known through its modern circulation in traditional session repertoires. It is typically played with a lively and rhythmic character well-suited to accompanying Irish step dancing.
