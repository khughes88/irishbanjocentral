Gale, The

Reel · 4/4 · A minor

The Gale is a modern 4/4 reel composed in A minor by American fiddler and composer Susan Conger. The tune features an upbeat, driving rhythm and a lyrical character typical of contemporary contra dance and New England style fiddle music. Because its history is primarily rooted in the modern traditional dance music revival, it is widely appreciated and performed within regional fiddle circles today.
