Tra An Phearla

Waltz · 3/4 · A minor

Tra An Phearla is a contemporary waltz composed by the renowned Irish fiddler Nollaig Casey. Set in three-quarter time and the key of A minor, the tune features a graceful, flowing melodic structure characteristic of modern Irish instrumental composition. It is frequently performed in contemporary traditional music circles as both a listening piece and a tune for couples dancing.
