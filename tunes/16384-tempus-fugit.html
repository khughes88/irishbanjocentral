Tempus Fugit

Waltz · 3/4 · E minor

Composed by Erwan Menguy, Tempus Fugit is a contemporary waltz written in the key of E minor with a standard 3/4 meter. The tune features a fluid melodic line that balances lyrical phrasing with a driving rhythmic pulse typical of modern French folk dance music. It is frequently performed in European balfolk settings where musicians emphasize its graceful yet energetic character.
