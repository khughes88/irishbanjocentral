Annam Cara

Jig · 6/8 · B minor

Composed by renowned flute and whistle player Michael McGoldrick, Annam Cara is a modern composition in the traditional Irish idiom. Set as a jig in six-eight meter and the key of B minor, it possesses an energetic and lyrical character typical of contemporary Celtic dance tunes. Because it is a modern piece, it does not carry the centuries-old historical associations of older traditional tunes, but it has become a popular item in contemporary sessions.
