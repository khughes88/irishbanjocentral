Johnny Doherty's

Reel · 4/4 · D major

Johnny Doherty's is a traditional Irish reel in 4/4 time and D major, named in honor of the renowned and influential Donegal fiddler Johnny Doherty. Because specific historical records regarding the exact composition date and original creator are uncertain, the tune is best known through the oral tradition and the playing style of Northwest Donegal musicians. Played with the characteristic drive and buoyant lift associated with the region's fiddling, it features an energetic rhythm well-suited for traditional step dancing.
