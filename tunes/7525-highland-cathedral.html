Highland Cathedral

March · 4/4 · G major

Highland Cathedral is a modern Scottish-style melody composed by Michael Korb and Ulrich Roever in 1982 for the Highland Games in Germany. Written in four-four meter and G major, it functions as a slow march characterized by its majestic, soaring melody and emotional resonance. The piece has since become a standard in the repertoire of bagpipe bands worldwide and is frequently performed at weddings, graduations, and state ceremonies.
