Jeremy And Regina's

Hornpipe · 4/4 · A minor

Jeremy And Regina's is a contemporary hornpipe composed in the 4/4 meter by fiddler Louise Bichan. Set in the key of A minor, the tune features a lively rhythm and a distinctively energetic character typical of modern Cape Breton and Scottish-influenced fiddling.
