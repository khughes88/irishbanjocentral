An Dro St Nazaire

Barndance · 4/4 · A minor

An Dro St Nazaire is a traditional tune performed in 4/4 meter rather than the typical duple-meter movement of a standard Breton an dro. Set in A minor, the melody shares rhythmic and structural similarities with traditional French and Celtic barndances. Because its exact historical origins and specific composer are not reliably documented, musicians typically interpret the piece with the steady, driving pulse characteristic of modern folk dance accompaniment.
