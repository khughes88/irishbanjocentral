Raheen, The

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

The traditional Irish reel known as The Raheen is played in 4/4 meter within the A dorian mode. Because its specific composer and exact historical origins are not recorded, it is primarily defined by its lively session style and rhythmic drive. Tunes of this regional character are standard fixtures in traditional repertoires across Ireland and are commonly performed at accelerated tempos for social dancing.
