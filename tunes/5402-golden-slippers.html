Golden Slippers

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Golden Slippers is a well-known traditional American tune derived from the 1879 minstrel song Oh, Dem Golden Slippers by James Bland. In traditional fiddle music, it is commonly played as a lively reel in 4/4 time and the key of G major. The tune features a bouncy, upbeat character and a rhythmic structure that makes it a enduring favorite for contra dances and square dances.
