Paddy G's

Hornpipe · 4/4 · G major

Paddy G's is a contemporary hornpipe in 4/4 meter and G major composed by Patrick Girardet. Because the tune is a modern composition rather than an older piece with traditional roots, its historical background is defined by its creation as part of the contemporary Celtic and folk repertoire. Played with the steady, bouncing rhythm characteristic of a hornpipe, it features a lively and articulate style suited to both dancing and instrumental performance.
