Four Strokes, The

Reel · 4/4 · D major

The Four Strokes is a modern reel composed by the prominent Scottish accordionist Phil Cunningham, set in the key of D major with a 4/4 meter. The tune features an upbeat, driving character and a rhythm well-suited to contemporary Scottish traditional music sessions.
