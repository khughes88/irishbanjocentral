Flooded Meadow, The

Reel · 4/4 · B dorian

The reel known as The Flooded Meadow was composed by the contemporary Scottish fiddler Duncan Chisholm. Set in B dorian and in 4/4 meter, the tune reflects a modern West Highland aesthetic characterized by sweeping, lyrical melodies and a driving yet expressive rhythmic pulse. While it lacks older historical associations, it has become a popular addition to contemporary traditional repertoires and session play.
