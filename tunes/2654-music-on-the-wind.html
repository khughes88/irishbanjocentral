Music On The Wind

Jig · 6/8 · D mixolydian

Music On The Wind is a 6/8 jig composed by the well-known Irish fiddle player Lucy Farr. Written in the D mixolydian mode, the tune features a lively and flowing rhythmic character typical of traditional Irish dance music.
