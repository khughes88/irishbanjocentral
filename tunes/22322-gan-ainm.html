Gan Ainm

Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Gan Ainm is an Irish traditional waltz in three-four time and the key of G major, with the title translating from Irish as "without name." Because the tune's specific provenance and exact date of composition are not documented, it exists as an anonymous part of the oral folk tradition. Musicians typically play it with a flowing, lyrical character that emphasizes the gentle, swaying pulse characteristic of a traditional waltz.
