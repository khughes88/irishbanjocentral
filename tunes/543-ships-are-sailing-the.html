Ships Are Sailing, The

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

The Ships Are Sailing is a well-known traditional Irish reel played in 4/4 meter and usually set in A dorian. Its exact geographical origin and composer remain unrecorded, as the tune was passed down through generations of traditional musicians before entering standard printed collections. Characterized by its flowing, sailing melodic contours and driving rhythm, it is typically performed at a brisk tempo in modern sessions.
