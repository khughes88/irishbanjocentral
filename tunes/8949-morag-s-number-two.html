Morag's Number Two

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Morag's Number Two is a contemporary reel in four-four time composed by Scottish fiddler Gavin Marwick. Written in the key of E minor, the tune features an energetic and driving character well-suited for Scottish country dancing and modern folk sessions. Because its history is primarily rooted in contemporary revival circles, it is most commonly performed with the lively ornamentation and rhythmic lift typical of contemporary fiddle playing.
