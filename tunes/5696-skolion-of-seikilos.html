Skolion Of Seikilos

Jig · 6/8 · D major

The Skolion of Seikilos is an ancient Hellenistic musical composition inscribed on a tombstone near Ephesus, representing one of the oldest surviving complete musical works in the world. Because its precise historical context remains uncertain beyond its ancient origins, musicologists focus on its lyrical memento mori theme reflecting the brevity of life. Although typically classified here with a jig meter and D major tonality to fit standard categorization, the original ancient melody follows a distinctive vocal style governed by ancient Greek musical notation rather than modern folk dance conventions.
