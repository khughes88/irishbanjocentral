Jig · 6/8 · D major

Maison De Glace is a modern jig composed in D major by accordionist Réjean Brunet of the prominent Quebecois traditional band La Bottine Souriante. The tune is written in 6/8 meter with a lively, rhythmic character that is well-suited for energetic contra dancing and French-Canadian step dancing.
