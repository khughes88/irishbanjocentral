Duke Of Fife's Welcome To Deeside, The

March · 4/4 · A major

The Duke Of Fife's Welcome To Deeside, The is a traditional march composed by the renowned Scottish fiddler James Scott Skinner. Written in 4/4 meter and set in the key of A major, the tune features an uplifting and regal character characteristic of Skinner's idiomatic style. Because precise historical associations beyond its dedication are uncertain, the piece remains best appreciated for its lively tempo and brilliant execution.
