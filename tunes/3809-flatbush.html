Flatbush

Waltz · 3/4 · G minor

Flatbush is a contemporary waltz in three-four meter composed by mandolin and clarinet virtuoso Andy Statman. Set in the key of G minor, the tune blends elements of traditional bluegrass with Jewish klezmer sensibilities in its distinctive melodic design. While it lacks a broader historical association beyond its modern composition, it is widely performed within contemporary acoustic and roots music circles.
