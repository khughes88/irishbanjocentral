Golden Keyboard, The

Reel · 4/4 · E dorian

Composed by the renowned accordion player Martin Mulhaire, The Golden Keyboard is a vibrant traditional Irish reel set in the key of E Dorian. Characterized by its lively 4/4 meter, the tune features an intricate and melodic phrasing that makes it a favorite in modern session playing. Because specific historical associations beyond its composition are scarce, the tune remains celebrated purely for its enduring technical brilliance and engaging musical structure.
