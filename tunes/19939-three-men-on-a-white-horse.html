Three Men On A White Horse

Reel · 4/4 · A major

Three Men On A White Horse is a traditional fiddle reel in 4/4 time and A major. Because the composer and exact historical origin of the tune are not documented, it circulates primarily through oral tradition among modern players. The melody is typically performed with a driving, upbeat rhythm and an energetic character well-suited for social dancing.
