Ian S. Robertson

March · 4/4 · G minor

Ian S. Robertson is a 4/4 march composed in G minor by the celebrated Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson. Because historical documentation regarding its exact date of composition is scarce, the tune is best defined by its driving rhythmic character and classic Shetland fiddle style. It remains a popular staple within contemporary Scottish and Shetland traditional repertoire, frequently performed by fiddle groups and soloists alike.
