Fermoy Lasses, The

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

The Fermoy Lasses is a well-known traditional Irish reel in four-four meter and E minor. Because its exact origin and composer are not historically documented, it circulates broadly within the traditional session repertoire as an anonymous tune. Characterized by its lively drive and energetic phrasing, it is typically performed at a brisk tempo suited for social dancing.
