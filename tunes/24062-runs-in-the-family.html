Runs In The Family

Reel · 4/4 · A dorian

Runs In The Family is a modern reel composed by Irish fiddler Siobhan Peoples. Written in the key of A dorian and set in 4/4 meter, the tune is played with the lively, driving rhythm characteristic of traditional Irish dance music. As a contemporary composition, it has quickly become a popular addition to modern sessions among players of traditional music.
