Slip jig · 9/8 · B minor

Kopanitsa is a traditional Bulgarian folk dance tune in nine-eight meter, characterized by an asymmetric rhythm divided into quick beats of two, two, two, and three. Because specific historical details and composers are not documented for most folk tunes of this type, its exact origin remains traditional. The music is typically performed in a lively and energetic style to accompany the fast-paced, intricate steps of the traditional Bulgarian dance of the same name.
