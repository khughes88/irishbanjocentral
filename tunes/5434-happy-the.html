Happy, The

Reel · 4/4 · G major

Composed by the renowned Irish-American button accordion player Joe Derrane, The Happy is a lively reel set in 4/4 time and the key of G major. The tune is characterized by its bright, uplifting melody and rhythmic drive that make it a favorite within traditional Irish dance music repertoires. Derrane recorded this upbeat composition during the mid-20th-century golden era of Irish accordion music in the United States, cementing its place as a celebrated modern classic.
