Neppendorfer Laendler

Mazurka · 3/4 · G major

Neppendorfer Laendler is a traditional folk tune associated with the Transylvanian Saxon community of Neppendorf, now known as Neaua in Romania, though its exact composer and date of origin remain unrecorded. Written in 3/4 time and G major, it functions as a mazurka with a lively, rustic character typical of rural dance repertoires from the region. The music is generally performed in a straightforward, traditional ensemble style that emphasizes its rhythmic bounce and communal dance heritage.
