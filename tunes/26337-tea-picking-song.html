Tea Picking Song

Polka · 2/4 · F major

Tea Picking Song is a traditional Chinese folk tune rooted in the agricultural traditions of tea-growing regions. Set in 2/4 meter and the key of F major, it is traditionally played as a polka with a lively, upbeat rhythm that mimics the brisk movements of harvesting tea leaves. Because its exact historical origins and composer are not historically documented, the piece is best known through its enduring presence in regional folk repertoire and dance arrangements.
