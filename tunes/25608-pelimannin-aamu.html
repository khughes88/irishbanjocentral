Pelimannin Aamu

Waltz · 3/4 · A minor

Pelimannin Aamu is a traditional-style Finnish waltz composed by the accordionist Antti Vesterinen in the mid-twentieth century. Written in three-four meter and set in A minor, the piece features a lyrical and nostalgic melody characteristic of Nordic folk accordion music. It remains a popular standard in the Finnish pelimanni fiddle and accordion repertoire, frequently performed at folk music gatherings and dance events.
