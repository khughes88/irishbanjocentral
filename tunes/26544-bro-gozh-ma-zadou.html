Bro Gozh Ma Zadoù

Waltz · 3/4 · D major

Bro Gozh Ma Zadoù is a traditional Breton anthem whose melody was composed by James James in 1856 and later paired with Breton lyrics by François Jaffrennou. Set in D major with a 3/4 meter, the tune has the gentle, flowing character of a waltz that lends itself well to communal singing. It serves as the recognized regional anthem of Brittany and holds deep cultural significance during regional gatherings and celebrations.
