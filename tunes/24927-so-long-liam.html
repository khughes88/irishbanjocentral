Strathspey · 4/4 · D major

So Long Liam is a contemporary Scottish strathspey in 4/4 time and D major composed by accordionist Phil Cunningham. The tune is written in a lively and expressive modern folk style that showcases the characteristic dotted rhythms and robust drive of the traditional genre.
