Farewell To Chernobyl

Reel · 4/4 · E minor

Farewell To Chernobyl is a contemporary reel composed in four-four time by Michel Ferry. Written in E minor, the tune is characterized by its driving rhythm and melancholic yet energetic modern character. Because specific historical associations beyond the composer's title are not well established, the piece is primarily celebrated today in Irish and contemporary Celtic music sessions for its dramatic melody and demanding fiddle technique.
