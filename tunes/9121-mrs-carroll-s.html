Mrs. Carroll's

Strathspey · 4/4 · A major

Mrs. Carroll's is a modern strathspey composed in A major by the acclaimed Irish-American fiddler Liz Carroll. Written in 4/4 meter, the tune features the driving rhythm, lifted dotted notes, and articulate bowing characteristic of the traditional Scottish and Cape Breton strathspey genre. Because it is a contemporary composition by a living master, it has no older historical associations, but it has become a popular staple in modern fiddle sessions worldwide.
