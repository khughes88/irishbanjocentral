Waltz · 3/4 · G major

Landler is a traditional Austrian and German folk dance in three-four meter that typically unfolds in major keys like G major. The dance is characterized by its lively, rustic waltz tempo and features couples spinning in energetic, rhythmic rotations. Because its exact historical origins and early composers remain unrecorded, the tune is best understood as a foundational precursor to the modern ballroom waltz.
