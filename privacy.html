Information and site operation

The site does not provide visitor accounts, contact forms, or user-submitted comments. Tune search runs in your browser using the tune data provided by the site. We do not use that search input to identify you.

You can also manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

This site links to external websites, including The Session and Google. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices, which we do not control.

Your privacy rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact blackcastlebanjo@gmail.com.
