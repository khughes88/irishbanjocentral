The instrument

A little history.
A lot of rhythm.

The tenor banjo found a lasting place in Irish traditional music for a reason: its clear, ringing tone can carry a melody through a lively session, while its short scale makes quick dance tunes feel right at home.

Whether you are choosing your first instrument, learning a reel, or looking to play with others, this is a place to get oriented and keep listening.
